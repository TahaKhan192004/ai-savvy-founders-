Purpose

Manual competitor research eats a full day and still misses the one thing that matters: the opening your competitor has left for you.

This SOP replaces that day of digging with a repeatable workflow. Claude, connected to Apify, reads a competitor across their website, social, sales pages, and podcast, then returns a structured brief. The final line surfaces the market gap that is hard to spot by hand, because you are usually too close to your own category to see it.

Who this is for

Founders, marketers, and operators who want fast, structured competitor intelligence without hiring an analyst or spending a day in browser tabs. No technical background is required. If you can paste a link and read a brief, you can run this.

Tools needed

  • A Claude account at claude.ai. Connectors are available on paid plans, so confirm your plan supports them.
  • An Apify account. A free account is enough to start. Apify runs on usage credits, so heavy scraping may need a paid tier.
  • One competitor to start with: a website URL, a social handle, or a brand name.
Note

Apify is a library of ready-made scrapers and automation tools called actors. Connected to Claude, it becomes the eyes: it pulls live data, and Claude reasons over what it finds.

01

Open Claude connectors

  1. Go to claude.ai and log in.
  2. Open Settings from the menu, then find the Connectors area.
  3. Select Add custom connector so you can point Claude at Apify directly.
02

Connect Apify

  1. In the connector dialog, paste the Apify MCP endpoint into the URL field.
  2. Give it a clear name like Apify so it is easy to spot later.
  3. Save, then authorize the connection. You will be sent to Apify to sign in and grant access, then returned to Claude.
Apify MCP endpoint https://mcp.apify.com
Important

You only do this once. After Apify is authorized it stays connected to your account. From then on you just switch it on inside the chat where you want to use it.

03

Start a fresh chat

  1. Open a new chat so the context is clean and focused on this one competitor.
  2. Confirm the Apify connector is toggled on for the conversation. Look for it in the tools or connectors control near the message box.
04

Add the competitor and the prompt

Paste one competitor, then the prompt below. Copy it as is, and swap in your competitor where prompted.

PromptYou are my competitor intelligence analyst. Use Apify to study this competitor across their website, their Instagram, their sales pages, and their podcast. Tell me: 1. How they position themselves 2. What they sell and at what price 3. The promises they keep making 4. The audience they are really speaking to 5. The gap they are leaving wide open for me Competitor: [PASTE COMPETITOR WEBSITE OR HANDLE HERE]
Tip

Keep the numbered structure. It tells Claude exactly what to return and keeps the brief scannable. The fifth line, the gap, is the payoff, so never drop it.

05

Let it run

  1. Send the prompt and watch Claude work. It will pick the right Apify actors and go read each source you named.
  2. If Claude asks to run a specific actor or needs a confirmation, approve it so it can pull live data.
  3. Give it a few minutes. It reads the website, the social profiles, the sales pages, and the podcast, then assembles the brief.
06

Read the gap

  1. Read the full teardown top to bottom. Positioning, offers, pricing, repeated promises, and true audience are all laid out.
  2. Go straight to the last section: the gap they are leaving open. This is the angle you can own that they are not defending.
  3. Turn the gap into action. Draft a positioning line, an offer, or a content angle that fills it before they do.
Note

Run this across three or four competitors and patterns appear fast: shared blind spots become your clearest opening, and repeated promises show you where the category is crowded.

Troubleshooting

ProblemLikely causeFix
Apify does not appear as a connector option Plan does not support connectors, or the custom connector was not saved Confirm your Claude plan includes connectors, then re-add the MCP endpoint and save before authorizing.
Claude replies without reading any live pages The Apify connector is toggled off for that chat Switch Apify on in the tools or connectors control near the message box, then resend the prompt.
A source comes back empty The page blocks scraping, or the URL or handle was wrong Double check the link, then ask Claude to try a different actor or skip that one source and continue.
The run stops partway Apify credits ran out mid-task Check your Apify usage. Top up credits or narrow the request to fewer sources per run.
The brief skips the market gap The fifth prompt line was removed or the competitor input was thin Restore the numbered structure and ask directly: what is the single gap this competitor leaves open.
