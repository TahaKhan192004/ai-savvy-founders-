02

How to use it

Five steps, start to finish

  1. Give Claude your site. The best way is Claude Code with your website's folder open, so Claude can read and edit the pages. New to it? See the Claude Code starter guide.
  2. Have your business details ready: your business name, contact details, what you sell, where you are based and where your customers are.
  3. Paste the prompt below and send it.
  4. Read each change before you approve it. Claude Code asks before it edits anything.
  5. Work through the lawyer flags. The prompt asks Claude to mark anything a lawyer should review. Send those pages to a lawyer before you rely on them.
If Claude can't edit your site

The prompt covers this. Claude writes each page in full and gives you a checklist of every change to make yourself, for example if your site is on a builder you cannot give it access to.

03

The prompt

Copy and paste

Prompt
I built this website and I want to make sure it isn't putting my business at legal risk. Please go through it and:

1. Add a privacy policy page, a terms and conditions page and a refund policy, written for my actual business and offers.
2. Check whether I need a cookie consent banner and add one if I do.
3. Add a clear consent checkbox to every form that collects someone's details.
4. Make sure I'm only collecting the data I actually use.
5. Check what my tracking (pixels, analytics) and third-party embeds (booking calendars, videos, chat widgets) are collecting, and tell me what I need to disclose.
6. Make the site accessible: alt text on every image, readable colour contrast, forms that work with a keyboard, and buttons that say what they do.
7. Flag any testimonial I can't prove a real client gave, and any claim or result I can't back up.
8. Add my real business name and contact details.
9. Check that I have the rights to every image.
10. Tell me which laws apply based on where I'm based and where my customers are.
11. Flag any other risk you notice.

If you can't edit the site directly, write each page in full and give me a checklist of every change to make myself. Mark anything a lawyer should review before I rely on it.
04

After you run it

Before you publish anything

  • Read every page Claude wrote. Check it describes what your business actually does and collects.
  • Fill in anything it flags as missing, such as your real name, address or contact details.
  • Remove or prove what it flags. A testimonial must come from a real client. A result or claim must be one you can back up.
  • Check your images. Replace any you do not have the rights to.
  • Have a lawyer review the terms and the refund policy if you are taking money, and anything else Claude marked for review.
  • Test it. Click the cookie banner, submit each form, and tab through the page using only the keyboard.
  • Update the policies later. If you add a new tracking tool, embed or form, run the prompt again.
Which laws apply

Claude will tell you which laws apply based on where you and your customers are. Use that as a starting point for the conversation with your lawyer, not as the final word.
