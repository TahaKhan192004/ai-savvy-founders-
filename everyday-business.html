Purpose and scope

AI for everyday business

This SOP shows you how to put an AI agent to work in eight common business types. Each playbook removes a specific daily bottleneck, such as missed calls, no-shows or slow follow-up.

Who this is for

  • Owners and operators of restaurants, gyms, online stores, auto shops, real estate teams, advisory practices, agencies and clinics
  • Team leads who will set up and check the agent

What you need before you start

  • Admin access to the software listed in your industry step
  • Your business hours, prices, policies and brand tone notes
  • A named person on your team who receives handoffs from the agent

How to use this SOP

  1. Find your industry in the table of contents and open that step.
  2. Tick each problem you recognise in the Confirm the problems checklist. Two or more ticks means the playbook fits.
  3. Work through the Deploy the agent list in order, one item at a time.
  4. Connect the software named in the Connect your software list.
  5. Run the test at the end of the step before you switch the agent on for customers.
Note

Your ticks in each checklist are saved for this browser session, so you can move between playbooks without losing your place.

FAQs

Common questions

What is an AI agent for a small business?

An AI agent is software that handles a repeatable business task on its own, such as answering calls, booking appointments, replying to leads or updating a CRM. It connects to the tools you already use.

Which industry playbook should I start with?

Start with the one that matches your business. Tick the problems you recognise in the checklist. Two or more ticks means the playbook fits.

Do I need to replace my current software?

No. Each playbook names the software the agent connects to, so it works alongside your existing tools.

What happens when a customer needs a human?

Set a handoff rule in every playbook. VIP customers, upset customers, and urgent or clinical questions should pass to your team.
