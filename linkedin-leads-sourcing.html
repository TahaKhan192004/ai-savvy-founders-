Find, Score, and Write to LinkedIn Leads

Purpose

Prospecting is the job nobody wants to do. Sitting on LinkedIn for two hours, opening profile after profile, reading their posts, hunting for something specific to say. This SOP hands all of that to Claude. It finds people who match your criteria, reads what they have been posting, scores how well they fit your ideal client profile, and writes each one a message anchored to something real rather than "I came across your profile."

You stay in control. Nothing sends without your approval. Claude does the research and the first draft. You are the yes or no.

Prerequisites

  • A Claude Project with two files: your ICP document and your voice document
  • The Apify MCP connector enabled
  • Your ideal client profile written out (job titles, industries, company size, signals)
01

Prepare your ICP and voice files

Create or open a Claude Project for prospecting. Load two documents:

  1. icp.md : Your ideal client profile. Include the job titles you are looking for, the industries, the company size range, geographic filters, and any signals that indicate a strong fit (recently hired, recently raised funding, recently posted about a relevant topic).
  2. voice.md : A short guide to how you write outreach. Include 2 to 3 examples of messages you have sent that felt right. Call out what to avoid (templates, "I came across your profile," stiff corporate language).

Claude reads both files before it searches or writes, so the output reflects your criteria and your tone from the start.

02

Connect Apify

If you have not already connected Apify, open the connectors panel in your project settings, search for Apify, and connect it. The LinkedIn search and post-fetching both run through Apify actors.

03

Run the LinkedIn search

Start a new chat and paste the search prompt. This tells Claude to use the harvestapi/linkedin-profile-search actor to find profiles matching your ICP. Keep the batch small (5 profiles is a good starting point).

Prompt Read icp.md and voice.md before doing anything else. Run harvestapi/linkedin-profile-search using the filters in icp.md, capped at 5 profiles. Pull each person's name, current company, role, location, and LinkedIn URL.

The search takes a few minutes because it queries LinkedIn through Apify. Let it run.

04

Pull their recent posts

Once the profiles come back, tell Claude to fetch their recent LinkedIn posts. This is what gives you something real to anchor each message on.

Prompt Fetch the last 5 posts from the past month for each person using harvestapi/linkedin-profile-posts. Save the batch so we can score and draft next.

NOTE

The post-fetching step costs a small amount of Apify credits per profile. This is why you run it in small batches (5 to 10 people) rather than firing at hundreds at once.

05

Score and draft outreach

Now Claude has both the profiles and their recent posts. This is the step where it scores each person against your ICP and writes a personalised message for each one.

Prompt For each lead in the batch, using their recent posts: 1. Score how well they fit icp.md from 0 to 100, with one line on why. 2. Write them a short outreach message in voice.md, anchored to one real, specific thing: a recent post of theirs, a role change, or a detail about their company. No generic openers. Do not make up anything that is not in the data. 3. Note which real detail you anchored the message on. Give me a table sorted by fit score, highest first: name, linkedin_url, current_company, role, fit_score, why, detail_anchored_on, message, status (pending review). Do not send anything.

The output is a table where every row has a score, a reason, a message, and the specific detail the message hooks onto. If someone has not posted recently, Claude will say so and lean on their role or company instead.

06

Review and export

Go through the table row by row. For each lead:

  1. Approve the message as-is
  2. Ask Claude to rewrite it with a different angle
  3. Drop the lead entirely

Once you have reviewed every row, export the approved ones.

Prompt Export the approved rows to a CSV, same columns, sorted by fit score highest first.

The CSV is ready to use however you send your outreach: paste into LinkedIn directly, import into a sequencing tool, or keep as a reference sheet.

WARNING

Claude does not send any messages. It generates the CSV for you to review. You are always the one choosing who gets contacted and when.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many leads should I run per batch?

Start with 5. Each profile's post history costs Apify credits, so small batches keep costs low. Once you are comfortable with the quality, you can bump it to 10 or 15 per run.

What if someone has not posted anything recently?

Claude will note that and anchor the message on their role, company, or a detail from their profile instead. It will not invent a post that does not exist.

Does this work for email outreach too?

The search and scoring are LinkedIn-specific, but you can modify the message drafting step to output cold emails instead of LinkedIn messages. Just update voice.md to include email examples.

Does Claude send the messages for me?

No. Claude generates a CSV of draft messages. You review and send them yourself. This is by design. You should always be the one deciding who to contact.
