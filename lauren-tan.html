What this talk is about

The big idea

Lauren Tan walks through how she works with AI agents day to day. The video description says she runs a team of 15+ GrokBot agents: a Chief of Staff agent, 3 managers, and 11 workers. The talk centers on building trust with agents so you can hand off bigger tasks without checking every step.

Topics covered include:

  • Reusable skills
  • Cloud agents
  • Working with multiple agents
  • Making a codebase easier for agents to work in
  • Verification systems that let agents confirm their own results
  • Her open-source framework, Pstack
Source of these details

The team size and the agent names come from the video description, as written by the channel. This page has not checked them. Watch the talk for her own account.
