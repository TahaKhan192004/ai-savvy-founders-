Start here

One prompt. One 15-second reel for your business.

Drop this prompt into Claude or Claude Code, fill in five details about your business, and it builds a vertical reel with big bold text, smooth motion and a sound on every move.

For best results

Choose Opus 5.5 as the model before you paste the prompt.

What you get

  • A 15-second vertical video (1080 x 1920, 30 fps) saved as myreel.mp4 in your folder.
  • Big, heavy text with the key word of each beat in your accent colour.
  • One hero element (a glowing ball, a line or a shape) that travels through every scene and carries the transitions.
  • Real motion on every change, never a plain fade: masks, wipes, letters popping in, shapes sweeping across.
  • Sound effects made for you (whoosh, pop, click, tick, soft thud), timed to the exact frame. No music, so you can add your own in Instagram.
  • Text kept in the safe zone, away from where Instagram's buttons and caption sit.
  • A live preview you can open to ask for changes.
01

Before you start

What you need

  • Claude or Claude Code. The prompt asks Claude to run commands and build the video in a folder, so Claude Code is the most reliable choice. In the Claude desktop app, open the Code tab and give it a new, empty folder. New to this? Start with the Claude Code starter guide.
  • Opus 5.5 selected as the model.
  • Node.js on your computer. If it isn't installed, Claude stops and tells you exactly what to download. Install it, then reply "done".
  • Five details about your business (Step 2 below). Two minutes to write.

03

The prompt

Copy and paste

Choose Opus 5.5, paste this into Claude or Claude Code, and send.

Prompt
Make me a 15-second reel for my business. Big bold text, smooth motion, a sound on every move.

ABOUT MY BUSINESS
Business name: [your business name]
What I do, in one line: [e.g. I help coaches fill their calendar without ads]
The one message this reel should land: [e.g. Your best clients are already in your inbox]
What people should do at the end: [e.g. Comment “CLIENTS” / Book a call at mysite.com]
Brand colours: [e.g. black and neon yellow, or your hex codes. Leave blank and pick a bold, high-contrast pair for me]

HOW TO WORK WITH ME
I’m not technical. Do all the setup and run every command yourself, and tell me in one plain sentence what you’re doing at each step. Build the video with Remotion in this folder. If Node.js isn’t installed, stop, tell me exactly what to download, and wait until I say “done”.

STEP 1: THE SCRIPT
Write the on-screen script first: 6 to 8 beats, no more than 6 words on screen at once, one idea per beat. Open with a hook that stops the scroll, build the message, and finish on a card with my business name and the call to action, held for at least 2.5 seconds. Show me the script and wait for my OK.

STEP 2: THE MOTION
Vertical 1080x1920, 30 fps, exactly 15 seconds.
Huge, heavy type (Inter Black from Google Fonts or similar), with the key word of each beat in the accent colour.
Every change of beat is a real move, never a plain fade: text rising from behind a mask, a wipe across the screen, letters popping in one by one, a shape sweeping across to reveal the next line.
Give the reel one hero element (a glowing ball, a line or a shape) that travels through every scene and carries the transitions.
Ease everything: ease-out for arrivals, ease-in-out for moves across the screen. Nothing linear, and no frame where nothing is moving.
Keep all text in the middle of the frame, away from the top 10%, the bottom 25% and the right edge, where Instagram’s buttons and caption sit.

STEP 3: THE SOUND
Make the sound effects yourself with code (short WAV files: whoosh, pop, click, tick, soft thud) so there’s nothing to download or license. Put a sound on every move, timed to the exact frame. No music, I’ll add that in Instagram.

STEP 4: CHECK, THEN RENDER
Before you finish, render a still from every beat and look at them yourself. Fix anything cut off, overlapping, or too small to read on a phone. Then render the final video as myreel.mp4 in this folder, tell me where it is, and show me how to open the live preview so I can ask for changes.

05

Make it yours

Ask for changes

When the first version is ready, Claude shows you how to open the live preview. Keep chatting in the same conversation and ask for changes in plain words. Some examples:

  • Make the hook faster and punchier.
  • Change the accent colour to [colour or hex code].
  • Slow down the last card so the call to action is easier to read.
  • Make the ball bigger and brighter.
  • Replace beat 3 with this line: [your line].
  • Make the text larger. It's hard to read on my phone.

Each time, ask Claude to re-render so myreel.mp4 is updated.

Before you post

  • Watch it on your phone, with the sound on.
  • Check every word, name and link in the text is correct.
  • Add your music in Instagram.
  • Add a caption that repeats the call to action.

The text is too small or cut offAsk Claude to make the text larger and keep it in the middle of the frame, then re-render
It feels slow or flatAsk for more motion on each beat and for the hero element to carry every transition
