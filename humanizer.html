Prerequisite

You need a Claude Pro, Team, or Enterprise account. Custom skills are not available on the free tier.

01

Download the Humanizer Skill

Download the humanizer_skill.zip file to your computer. Save it somewhere you can find it easily (your Desktop or Downloads folder works fine).

humanizer_skill.zip

Zip archive · ~40 KB · Contains SKILL.md + reference files

Download Skill →
Note

Do not unzip the file. Claude accepts the .zip format directly during upload.

02

Open Claude

Go to claude.ai in your browser and log in with your account.

Make sure you are on a Pro, Team, or Enterprise plan. You can check your plan by clicking your profile icon in the bottom-left corner.

03

Open the Customize Panel

Click the icon in the bottom-left corner of the Claude interface. In the menu that appears, click Customize.

This opens a panel where you can manage your profile, preferences, and installed skills.

Screenshot: The Customize option in the Claude sidebar menu
04

Navigate to Skills

Inside the Customize panel, click the Skills tab.

This is where all your custom skills live. If this is your first skill, the list will be empty.

05

Upload the Skill

  1. Click the Add Skill button
  2. Select Upload Skill from the options
  3. Browse to the humanizer_skill.zip file you downloaded in Step 1 and select it
  4. Wait for the upload to complete. Claude will process the skill files automatically.
Screenshot: The skill upload dialog in Claude's Customize panel
06

Verify and Start Using

After the upload finishes, the Humanizer skill should appear in your skill list. Confirm it shows as active.

To use it, open any conversation and ask Claude to humanize a piece of text. You can say things like:

  1. "Humanize this draft for me"
  2. "Strip the AI tone from this paragraph"
  3. "Review this text for AI patterns"
  4. "Rewrite this so it sounds like a person wrote it"
Tip

For the best results, paste your own previous writing as a voice sample. The skill will match your sentence length, vocabulary, and style preferences from that sample.

What You Get

What the Humanizer Skill Does

The Humanizer detects and rewrites 36 documented AI writing patterns, drawn from Wikipedia's "Signs of AI writing" catalog. It covers eight categories of tells that make text sound machine-generated.

Vocabulary Cleanup

Catches overused AI words like "delve," "tapestry," "landscape," and "crucial." Replaces them with plain, natural alternatives.

Structure Rewrite

Breaks apart formulaic outlines, dictionary-definition leads, forced triple groupings, and monotone paragraph rhythms.

Tone Matching

Matches the register of your context. A Slack message stays casual. A board memo stays formal. A blog post gets personality.

Fact Preservation

The skill's first rule: keep every claim. It rewrites tone and rhythm without dropping, adding, or softening any facts from your original.

Capabilities

What It Catches

The skill checks against eight groups of AI writing patterns. Here is a sample of the most common ones it fixes.

  • Inflated importance and legacy language ("serves as," "is a testament to," "marks a pivotal shift")
  • Trailing -ing clauses that fake analysis ("...underscoring its commitment to excellence")
  • Sales language in non-sales contexts ("vibrant," "renowned," "groundbreaking," "breathtaking")
  • Vague sources ("experts say," "industry reports indicate," "critics argue")
  • Copula avoidance ("serves as," "functions as," "represents" where "is" works)
  • Forced triples and filler paragraphs about "broader trends" or "enduring significance"
  • Chatbot residue: greetings, sign-offs, knowledge-cutoff disclaimers, and flattery
  • Em dashes, synonym cycling, and monotone sentence rhythm
