Purpose

This is not one new SOP. It is a map of ten AI systems already documented on this site, each one built from Claude plus a connector or two. Every entry below is a one-line description, the exact setup stack, and a link to the full step-by-step SOP.

Who this is for: Founders who want to see the whole shape of what is possible before committing to one build.

How to use this page: Read the ten, find the one that maps to whatever is eating your week right now, click through, and build it. Then come back for the next one.

LinkedIn Lead Engine
