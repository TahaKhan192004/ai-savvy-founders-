Which Claude, when

A plain guide to choosing between Sonnet, Opus and Fable, and what to check when Claude seems to be rushing.

The short answer

Use Sonnet for everyday work. Switch to Opus when there is more to think through. Use Fable to build an app that does a job for your business. If Claude seems to be rushing, turn the effort setting up.

01

Keep Sonnet open for the day to day

Sonnet is quick and does what you need, so it is the one to keep open for most of the day.

Reach for Sonnet when you are

  1. Writing a message
  2. Pulling the main points out of a long document
  3. Planning your week
  4. Working through a small task where you want an answer fast
Tip
If the answer comes back good, stay on Sonnet. Switching up is for jobs that need more thought.
02

Switch to Opus when there is more to think through

Opus is where you want Claude to take more time with the details.

Reach for Opus when you are

  1. Designing a website
  2. Editing a video
  3. Researching a topic
  4. Figuring out what to charge

To switch models

  1. Find the model name at the bottom right of the message box, for example Sonnet 5
  2. Click it and choose Opus
  3. Send your message as normal
Note
You can switch models in the middle of a conversation, so there is no need to start a new chat.
03

Use Fable to build an app that does a job for your business

Fable is the one to use when you want an app that does a repeat job for you. A good example is an app that reads new enquiries, checks whether each one is a good fit and gets a reply ready for you.

Describe the job in three parts

  1. Say what comes in. For example: new enquiries from your website form
  2. Say what to check. For example: whether the enquiry is a good fit for your business
  3. Say what you want back. For example: a reply that is ready for you to review
Important
The app gets the reply ready. You decide what gets sent.
04

Check the effort setting when Claude seems to be rushing

If an answer feels thin or hurried, the model is often fine and the effort setting is what needs a nudge. A higher setting gives Claude more time to work through what you asked.

  1. Look at the bottom right of the message box, next to the model name
  2. Read the current effort level. For example: High
  3. Turn the effort up
  4. Send the same message again and compare the two answers
Tip
Try the effort setting before you switch models. It is the quicker fix.
05

Run the ten second check before you send

Tap each line that matches your job. Your ticks stay while this tab is open.

Questions

Quick answers

Which Claude model should I use every day?

Sonnet. It is quick and handles everyday work such as writing a message, pulling the main points from a long document and planning your week.

When should I switch to Opus?

Switch when there is more to think through, such as designing a website, editing a video, researching a topic or working out what to charge. Opus takes more time with the details.

What is Fable best for?

Building an app that does a job for your business, such as reading new enquiries, checking whether each one is a good fit and getting a reply ready for you.

What should I do if Claude seems to be rushing?

Check the effort setting next to the model name and turn it up. A higher setting gives Claude more time to work through what you asked.
