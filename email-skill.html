WHY THIS EXISTS

The problem isn't that you don't know you should email your list.

Most founders know they should email more. What stops them is the blank screen.

You sit down to write, you have nothing, and an hour later you close the laptop having sent nothing.

This skill fixes the part that actually stalls you. You tell Claude what you're selling, and it builds the email around a real story, the same way the emails you actually open and read are built.

WHAT IT DOES

Four things, every time.

01

Finds the angle

It pulls a small everyday moment and connects it to your point and your call to action, instead of opening with a generic pitch.

02

Writes subject lines that get opened

You get options built to be tested, written so they don't sit unread in the inbox.

03

Writes the full body

The structure is built to earn the open, hold the read, and earn the click, start to finish.

04

Handles more than one email

Welcome sequences for new subscribers, re-engagement emails for a list that's gone cold, and full makeovers when you paste in an email you already have.

Important

The one thing it does not do is impose a voice on you. It gets the structure and the psychology right, then hands it back so you can run it through your own voice. Every email still sounds like you.

SETUP

How to install

Six steps, done once, inside claude.ai.

01

Go to claude.ai

Open claude.ai in your browser and sign in.

02

Open Customise

Find and open the Customise menu.

03

Go to Skills

Inside Customise, open the Skills section.

04

Click the plus icon

Click the plus icon to add a new skill.

05

Choose upload from computer

Select the option to upload a skill file directly from your device.

06

Upload the skill file

Upload the file you downloaded above. It will start appearing in your skills right away.
