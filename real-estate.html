Purpose

What happens to your leads after the first call?

In a typical brokerage, a new inquiry waits for an agent, an old lead sits untouched in the CRM, and follow-up depends on who remembers. This SOP sets up 12 AI systems that handle that work in the background, so agents spend their time on viewings and deals.

How many old leads are sitting untouched in your CRM right now?
Key takeaways
  • Every new inquiry gets an automated reply in under one minute, with viewing slots offered from the agent's real calendar.
  • Leads are routed and scored by your own brokerage rules, so hot buyers reach the right agent with a one-line brief.
  • Buyers receive matching listings automatically, and the system learns from what they open and reject.
  • Agents approve anything that carries risk: listing facts, pricing, document checks and outbound follow-ups.
  • Build in four phases. Systems 01 and 03 come first because every other system depends on clean lead data.

Who this is for

  • Brokerage owners and team leads
  • Sales managers who own lead response
  • Operations or CRM admins doing the setup

What you will have

  • Instant, qualified replies to new leads
  • Old leads segmented and re-engaged
  • A live view of response time and conversion

00
Before any system

Prepare the foundation

Every system reads from and writes to your CRM. If the fields are messy, the automations will be too. Spend the first week here.

  1. Export your full lead list from the CRM and remove duplicates by phone number and email.
  2. Create or clean the required fields listed in the table below. Make Intent, Areas and Budget dropdowns, not free text.
  3. Standardise area names into one list, and map spelling variants to it (for example, three versions of the same neighbourhood become one value).
  4. Write your brokerage rules in a one-page document:
    • Working hours and the response time you promise
    • Which agent or team covers which areas and budget bands
    • Who gets a lead when no rule matches
  5. Record consent per channel (SMS, email, WhatsApp) on every contact. Contacts without consent stay out of systems 02 and 08.
  6. Connect each agent's calendar to the CRM and create a Viewing appointment type with travel buffers.

01
Capture and respond

Lead to viewing

Replies to inquiries in seconds, asks requirements, offers viewing slots and updates the CRM.

Trigger: new inquiry from any source Forms, SMS, email, CRM, calendar
  1. Connect every lead source (website forms, portals, ad forms, inbound SMS) to the CRM so each inquiry creates or updates a contact with a Lead source tag.
  2. Build one entry workflow that fires on new inquiry. Use a single workflow for all sources so a lead never gets two first replies.
  3. Send the first reply within 60 seconds on the channel the lead used, mentioning the listing or area they asked about.
  4. Let the AI assistant ask qualifying questions one at a time (use the prompt below), and write each answer into the matching CRM field.
  5. Once area, budget and timeline are known, pull the next available Viewing slots from the assigned agent's calendar and offer two or three options.
  6. When the lead picks a slot, create the appointment, move the lead to the Viewing booked stage and notify the agent.
  7. Send reminders 24 hours and 2 hours before the viewing, with the address and agent name.
  8. If the lead stops replying for 30 minutes during working hours, create a call task for the agent with the conversation so far.

Qualifying assistant prompt

Prompt
You are the inquiry assistant for {{brokerage_name}}.
A new lead has contacted us about {{listing_or_area}}.

Goal: collect their requirements and book a viewing.

Rules:
- Reply in the lead's language. Keep each message under 300 characters.
- Ask one question at a time, in this order:
  buy or rent, preferred areas, budget, bedrooms, move-in timeline.
- Never state prices, availability or fees that are not in {{listing_data}}.
- When you know area, budget and timeline, offer the slots in {{available_slots}}.
- If the lead asks for a person, is a seller or landlord, or sounds upset,
  reply: "Thanks, I'm passing you to {{agent_name}} now." and stop.

Return two things after each turn:
1. message: the text to send
2. fields: {"intent":"","areas":[],"budget_min":null,"budget_max":null,
   "bedrooms":null,"timeline":"","ready_to_book":false,"handoff":false}
Done when

A test inquiry gets a reply in under a minute, the booked viewing appears in the agent's calendar, and the CRM fields are filled.

02
Capture and respond

Dormant lead reactivation

Segments old leads, sends relevant messages and routes replies to an agent.

Trigger: weekly batch CRM, SMS, email
  1. Define dormant: no two-way contact for 90 days or more, not marked closed, and not opted out.
  2. Build segments from your CRM fields:
    • Buyers by area and budget band
    • Renters with a lease ending soon
    • Past sellers and landlords
    • Leads who viewed a property but did not offer
  3. Write one message per segment that references something specific to them, such as a new listing in their area or a recent price change on a property they viewed.
  4. Send in small daily batches (start with 50 per day) to protect your sender reputation.
  5. Pass every reply through the classifier prompt below and tag the contact with the result.
  6. Route interested and question replies to the assigned agent with a task and a one-line summary. Mark unsubscribe as do not contact immediately.

Reply classifier prompt

Prompt
Classify this reply to a reactivation message from a real estate brokerage.
Return JSON only.

Categories:
interested | not_now | already_bought_or_rented | wrong_contact | unsubscribe | question

{
  "category": "",
  "summary": "one line in plain English",
  "follow_up_date": "YYYY-MM-DD or null"
}

Reply: {{reply_text}}
Tip

Messages that mention a real listing or a real number get far more replies than a general check-in. Pull the listing link from your inventory source into the message.

Done when

A test batch of 20 contacts is sent, each reply is tagged correctly, and interested replies create agent tasks.

03
Capture and respond

Lead routing

Assigns leads by budget, area and intent using your brokerage rules.

Trigger: lead qualified in system 01 CRM, SMS or app notifications
  1. Turn the rules document from Step 00 into a routing table. Rules run top to bottom and the first match wins.
  2. Inside each team, use round robin so leads spread evenly across available agents.
  3. Set a fallback agent for leads that match no rule.
  4. Notify the assigned agent with a short brief: name, intent, area, budget, timeline and the last message.
  5. Add a response limit: if the agent has not contacted the lead within 15 minutes in working hours, reassign and alert the sales manager.

04
Match buyers

Buyer-property matching

Compares buyer requirements with inventory and sends matching listings.

Trigger: new listing, or daily run Inventory source, CRM, SMS or email
  1. Keep inventory in one structured source with these fields: price, area, bedrooms, property type, status and listing link.
  2. Filter active buyers on hard criteria first: area, bedrooms and budget within 10 percent.
  3. Rank the remaining listings on soft criteria the buyer mentioned, such as view, floor or parking.
  4. Send up to three matches with listing links, and log each sent listing on the contact so nothing is sent twice.
  5. When a new listing goes live, run it against all active buyers the same day.
Done when

A new test listing reaches only the buyers whose requirements match, and the send is logged on each contact.

05
Agent productivity

Agent follow-up copilot

Takes a voice note after a call and produces CRM notes, tasks and a follow-up draft.

Trigger: agent submits a voice note Phone or app, transcription, CRM
  1. Give agents one place to drop a voice note after a call: a WhatsApp number, a mobile form or the CRM app. The note must include the lead's name or phone number.
  2. Transcribe the note automatically.
  3. Run the transcript through the prompt below to extract the summary, field changes, tasks and a follow-up draft.
  4. Write the note to the contact timeline, update changed fields and create tasks with due dates.
  5. Send the follow-up draft to the agent for approval. The agent edits if needed and sends.

Voice note to CRM prompt

Prompt
Turn this agent call note into CRM updates.
Use only facts stated in the transcript. If a value is not mentioned, return null.

Return JSON:
{
  "note": "3 to 5 sentence summary of the call",
  "field_updates": {"budget_max": null, "areas": [], "bedrooms": null, "timeline": null},
  "tasks": [{"title": "", "due_date": "YYYY-MM-DD"}],
  "follow_up_draft": "message under 400 characters, written in the agent's voice"
}

Agent: {{agent_name}}
Lead: {{lead_name}}
Transcript: {{transcript}}
Note

Keep the agent approval step. The draft saves typing time, and the agent stays in control of what the client receives.

Done when

A test voice note creates a timeline note, updates the budget field and adds a dated task within two minutes.

07
Advanced systems

Listing photo-to-copy

Reads listing photos, tags rooms, finishes and light, flags missing room shots and drafts portal-ready descriptions and captions in the listing record.

Trigger: photos uploaded to a listing Listing record, vision model
  1. Agent uploads photos to the listing record along with the fact fields: size, bedrooms, bathrooms, price and permit or reference number.
  2. The vision model tags each photo by room type, finishes, natural light and view.
  3. Compare tags against your required shot list (living room, kitchen, each bedroom, each bathroom, exterior or view, floor plan) and flag what is missing.
  4. Draft a portal description within the portal's character limit, plus a short social caption, using the fact fields and photo tags only.
  5. Save both drafts to the listing record with status Pending review. The agent approves before anything is published.
Warning

Size, price, permit numbers and amenities must come from the listing fields, never from the photos. Instruct the model to leave out any fact it cannot find in the record.

Done when

A test listing with one bedroom photo missing gets flagged, and the draft description uses only facts from the record.

08
Advanced systems

Buyer-match agent

Learns from which listings a buyer opens or rejects, ranks live inventory, sends the top three by WhatsApp and logs each reaction in the CRM.

Builds on: system 04 WhatsApp Business, inventory, CRM
  1. Track buyer reactions to every listing sent in system 04: link opened, viewing requested, saved or rejected.
  2. When a buyer rejects a listing, ask one short question about why (too small, wrong area, price, layout) and store the answer.
  3. Update the buyer's preference weights from these reactions. A buyer who rejects three ground-floor units moves floor level up their list.
  4. Re-rank live inventory for that buyer and send the top three by WhatsApp using an approved message template.
  5. Log each send and each reaction on the contact timeline so the agent sees the full history before a call.
Important

WhatsApp messages you start outside a 24-hour conversation window need a pre-approved template. Submit your listing-send template early, as approval can take time.

Done when

A test buyer who rejects two listings for size receives a new top three ranked toward larger units.

09
Advanced systems

Property and project Q&A

Answers buyer questions from brochures, floor plans, payment plans and developer FAQs with the source page cited, and routes unknowns to an agent.

Trigger: buyer question on chat or WhatsApp Document knowledge base
  1. Collect the documents for each project or listing into one folder: brochure, floor plans, payment plan and developer FAQ. Use the latest version only.
  2. Run text recognition on scanned PDFs so every page is searchable.
  3. Load the documents into a knowledge base, tagging each chunk with project name, document name and page number.
  4. Connect the Q&A assistant to your chat channel using the prompt below. It answers only from the documents and cites the source.
  5. When the answer is not in the documents, the assistant says so and creates an agent task with the exact question.
  6. Review the unanswered questions log weekly and add the missing documents.

Project Q&A prompt

Prompt
You answer buyer questions for {{brokerage_name}} about {{project_name}}.

Use only the documents provided below.
After each answer, cite the source as (Document name, page X).

If the answer is not in the documents, reply:
"I'll check that with the team and come back to you shortly."
and set "route_to_agent": true.

Never estimate prices, fees, handover dates, rental yields or returns.

Documents:
{{retrieved_chunks}}

Buyer question: {{question}}
Done when

Ten test questions return answers with correct page citations, and a question with no answer in the documents creates an agent task.

Lead scoring model

Scores each lead on source, budget signals, replies and response speed, then routes hot leads to the right agent with a one-line brief.

Trigger: any new lead event Feeds system 03
  1. Create a Lead score number field and a Lead temperature dropdown (hot, warm, cold).
  2. Start with the point values in the table below. Adjust them to your brokerage after the first month.
  3. Recalculate the score each time a new event happens: a reply, a booking, a missed follow-up.
  4. When a lead becomes hot, send it through system 03 with a one-line brief, for example: "Cash buyer, 2 bed in your area, replied in 4 minutes, wants to view this week."
  5. Each month, compare scores against closed deals and raise the weight of the signals that predicted a sale.

11
Advanced systems

Deal document reader

Reads passport, title deed and tenancy scans into the deal record and flags expiries and name mismatches for a human check.

Trigger: document uploaded to a deal Secure storage, text recognition, CRM
  1. Add a secure upload field to each deal record. Documents go there and nowhere else.
  2. Run text recognition to extract key values:
    • Passport: full name, number and expiry date
    • Title deed: owner name, property reference and plot or unit number
    • Tenancy contract: tenant and landlord names, start and end dates, rent
  3. Compare names across all documents and the CRM contact. Flag any mismatch, including spelling differences.
  4. Flag any ID that expires within 60 days or before the expected transfer date.
  5. Create a Document check task for the deal owner. Extracted values stay in draft until a person confirms them.
Warning

ID documents are sensitive personal data. Store them with restricted access, never forward them through chat, and follow the data protection law that applies to your brokerage.

Done when

A test deal with a misspelled buyer name and a passport expiring next month raises both flags and a check task.

12
Advanced systems

Pricing and market dashboard

Combines comparable sales, days on market and price changes, flags overpriced listings and shows a suggested price range for agent review.

Refresh: weekly Your deal history, licensed market data
  1. Gather your data sources: your own closed deals, comparable sales from official or licensed sources, and your active listings.
  2. For each active listing, track days on market and every price change.
  3. Group comparables by area, property type, bedrooms and size band.
  4. Flag listings priced above the comparable range, or with days on market above the area median.
  5. Show a suggested price range for each flagged listing, with the comparables used listed underneath so the agent can check them.
Note

The suggested range is input for the agent's conversation with the seller. It is not a formal valuation and should not be shared as one.

Done when

Agents can see every active listing with its comparable range, days on market and a flag where action is needed.

FAQs

Questions brokers ask

Which AI system should a real estate brokerage build first?

Start with lead to viewing (01) and lead routing (03). Together they make sure every new inquiry gets a reply in under a minute and lands with the right agent. Every other system depends on that data being clean.

Do these AI systems replace real estate agents?

No. The systems handle first replies, data entry, matching and reminders. Agents still run viewings, negotiate, check documents and approve pricing and listing copy.

What CRM does a brokerage need for these systems?

Any CRM with custom fields, pipeline stages, calendar booking and webhooks or an API. GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Follow Up Boss, Salesforce and Zoho all work.

How fast should a new real estate lead get a reply?

The automated reply should go out in under one minute. A hot lead should hear from a human agent within 15 minutes during working hours.

Can a brokerage message old leads on WhatsApp or SMS?

Only contacts who gave consent for that channel. WhatsApp messages you start outside the 24-hour window also need an approved message template.

How long does it take to set up all 12 systems?

Plan for 6 to 10 weeks in four phases. The first two systems can be live within two weeks if the CRM data is already in order.
