Launch your first ChatGPT ad preview

Quick answer

To create a ChatGPT ad preview, go to ads.openai.com, click Start now, sign up, enter your website, and click Create ad preview. Your site is used to put together a suggested ad and campaign. Click Review details to edit your budget, location and landing page.

Why this matters

Reach shoppers while they decide

If you sell products, ChatGPT ads could be a real opportunity this holiday season.

  • Someone asks ChatGPT to find running shoes for their first 5K. They compare options and work out which pair to buy.
  • Your product can show up right below the answer, with an image and a link to shop.
  • You reach that person while they are actively deciding what to spend their money on.

This guide shows how quickly you can get a first ad preview.

Before you start

What you need ready

  • A website that sells your products
  • The full address of that website, for example https://yourstore.com
  • An email address you can use to sign up
  • A test budget in mind and the locations where you ship
  • About ten minutes of focused time
01

Open the ads page

  1. Open a new tab in your browser.
  2. Go to the address below, or click the link.
OpenAI Ads ads.openai.com
  1. Wait for the page to load until you see the Start now button.
Note

Ad products from OpenAI are new, so screens and button labels can change. If a label differs slightly from this guide, follow the closest option on screen.

02

Click Start now and sign up

  1. Click Start now.
  2. Follow the sign-up prompts on screen.
  3. Finish sign-up until you reach the screen that asks for your website.
03

Enter your website

  1. Find the website field.
  2. Type the full address of your online store, starting with https://.
  3. Read the address once more to catch any typo before you continue.
Tip

The suggested ad is put together from your site. Before you start, make sure your product pages are live and show clear images, so the preview has good material to work with.

04

Create your ad preview

  1. Click Create ad preview.
  2. Wait while your site is used to put together a suggested ad and campaign.
  3. Look at the preview when it appears.
Important

Treat this first preview as a suggestion. Read all of it before you change anything.

05

Review the suggested ad

  1. Check the image shown with the ad.
  2. Read the ad text.
  3. Confirm the link points to a page you want shoppers to see.
  4. Write down anything you want to change, so you can fix it in the next step.
06

Click Review details and edit

  1. Click Review details.
  2. Edit your budget to the amount you want to test with.
  3. Edit your location to match where your customers live and where you ship.
  4. Edit the page you are sending people to so it matches the ad.
SettingWhat it controlsWhat to check
BudgetHow much you plan to spend on the campaignPick a test amount you are comfortable with
LocationWhere your ad can reach peopleMatch the areas you actually ship to
Landing pageThe page shoppers reach after they clickChoose a page that matches the product in the ad
Warning

Read the budget field twice before you move on. It sets how much you plan to spend.

07

Decide when to test

  1. Ask yourself whether your customers use ChatGPT to decide what to buy.
  2. If they do, plan your test before the holiday rush.
  3. Put a date in your calendar for when you will review the results.

Verify it worked

You are done when

  • You signed up at ads.openai.com
  • Your website is entered and the ad preview is on screen
  • Your budget, location and landing page are reviewed and edited
  • You have a plan to test before the holiday rush

Next steps

Keep the momentum

  1. Run your first ad preview this week so you have time to adjust before the holiday rush.
  2. Compare the suggested landing page with your best selling product page.
  3. Repeat the process for each product line you want to test.

FAQ

Common questions

What is a ChatGPT ad preview?

It is a suggested ad and campaign put together from your website. The ad can show up below an answer in ChatGPT with an image and a link to shop.

Where do I create a ChatGPT ad preview?

Go to ads.openai.com, click Start now, sign up, enter your website, then click Create ad preview.

What can I edit before the campaign is set?

Click Review details. You can edit your budget, your location and the page you are sending people to.

Who should test ChatGPT ads?

Anyone who sells products and whose customers use ChatGPT to decide what to buy. Testing before the holiday rush is the recommended timing.

How long does it take to get a first ad preview?

Plan on about ten minutes. The preview is generated automatically from your website once you click Create ad preview.
