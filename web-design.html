5 skills that turn Claude into a premium web designer

Drop these five skills into Claude and stop getting websites that look like every other AI generated site. Each one fixes a specific gap: taste, live editing, testing, motion, and 3D.

5 minute read 5 skills covered Updated September 2026

Each of these is a Claude skill: a folder of rules and reference material that Claude reads before it builds anything, so it follows a method instead of guessing at what "good design" means. Save this page and work through the five below in order, or pick the one that fixes what is bothering you most right now.

01

Taste

Gives Claude actual design rules to follow instead of its own defaults, which is why AI built sites tend to look the same. With this skill in place, layout, spacing, and type choices start following a real point of view rather than a template.

Leonxlnx / taste-skill github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill
02

Impeccable

Helps Claude review and improve a site that already exists, and pairs it with a live editor so changes can be made directly in the browser instead of round tripping through chat every time.

pbakaus / impeccable github.com/pbakaus/impeccable
03

Playwright CLI

Lets Claude open multiple browsers at once, click through a site the way a visitor would, and take screenshots to confirm layout and functionality actually hold up instead of just looking right in one screenshot.

microsoft / playwright-cli github.com/microsoft/playwright-cli
04

Emil Design Eng

Built on Emil Kowalski's approach to design engineering, from his time at Vercel and Linear. It pushes Claude toward smooth, restrained motion instead of static pages or motion that feels bolted on.

emilkowalski / skills github.com/emilkowalski/skills (emil-design-eng)
05

3D Front-End

For homepages that feel like flying through a room as you scroll. Handles scroll-driven 3D sites, room walkthroughs, and fly-through experiences in a single build.

zyliu0 / 3d-frontend github.com/zyliu0/3d-frontend
Note
These skills give Claude a stronger starting point and a way to check its own work. They do not replace a founder or designer making the final call on brand, content, and what the site is actually there to do.
