01

Before you start

What you need

  • Claude Pro, with Fable 5.2 available in the model picker.
  • 30 to 45 minutes without interruptions. The best answers come when you are not rushed.
  • Your real numbers to hand: prices, rough revenue, hours per week, team size. Ranges are fine.
  • A place to save the results, such as a notes app or document.
Privacy

Do not paste passwords, account numbers or other people's private details. Rough numbers and ranges are enough.

02

The process

Five steps, start to finish

  1. Open Claude and pick Fable 5.2 in the model picker.
  2. Paste the prompt below and send it. Fable asks its first question.
  3. Answer one question at a time. Tell it everything: what you sell, who buys it, what is eating your week, and where you want to be a year from now. Correct it when it reflects things back wrongly.
  4. Take the two outputs when the interview ends: your custom instructions and your 12-month plan.
  5. Paste the custom instructions into your Claude settings and save the plan somewhere you will see it.
03

The prompt

Copy and paste

Prompt
I want you to act as a sharp business strategist and interviewer. Your goal is to understand my business deeply, then write (1) custom instructions I'll paste into my Claude settings so every future chat starts with full context on me, and (2) a step-by-step plan to get me where I want to be in 12 months.

HOW TO RUN THE INTERVIEW
- Ask me ONE question at a time. Wait for my answer before asking the next.
- Ask follow-ups when my answers are vague, generic, or contradict something I said earlier. Push for specifics: numbers, names, examples.
- Briefly reflect back what you heard every 5–6 questions so I can correct you.
- Aim for roughly 20–30 questions, but stop when you have enough. Don't pad.
- Don't give advice during the interview. Just gather.

COVER THESE AREAS (in whatever order flows naturally)
1. Me: my role, background, strengths, weaknesses, how I like to work and communicate, how many hours I have per week.
2. The business: what I sell, pricing, delivery model, how long I've been at it, current revenue/profit (ranges are fine), team size.
3. Customers: who buys, why they buy, where they find me, my best and worst customers, common objections.
4. Marketing and sales: channels I use, what's working, what isn't, my content and offers.
5. Operations: what eats my week, repetitive tasks, bottlenecks, tools and software I use.
6. Competition and positioning: who else they could buy from, why they'd choose me.
7. Goals: where I want to be in 12 months (revenue, lifestyle, team, offers), and what "success" means to me personally.
8. Constraints: budget, time, skills I lack, fears, things I've tried that failed.
9. How I want Claude to help: tone, format, level of detail, what to push back on, what to never do.

WHEN THE INTERVIEW IS DONE, produce two outputs:

OUTPUT 1: CUSTOM INSTRUCTIONS
- Written in second person to Claude ("The user is…", "When helping them…").
- Include: who I am, the business, the customers, current goals, key constraints, my preferred tone and format, and specific do's and don'ts.
- Dense and specific. No fluff. Keep it short enough to fit a settings box (aim for under 1,500 characters; give a longer version too if needed).

OUTPUT 2: 12-MONTH PLAN
- Start with a one-paragraph diagnosis: the 2–3 biggest levers and the biggest risk.
- Break it into quarters, then into monthly milestones.
- For the first 30 days, give weekly actions concrete enough that I know exactly what to do Monday morning.
- For each milestone: the goal, the actions, the metric that proves it worked, and roughly how much time it'll take.
- Flag what to stop doing, and what to automate or delegate.
- End with the 3 things to do this week.

How to answer well

Give specifics: numbers, names and examples. "Sales are slow" gives Fable little to work with. "I get about 20 leads a month and close 3" gives it something real. It will push you for specifics, so answer honestly.

06

After the window closes

What keeps working

  • Your custom instructions. They live in your settings, so every new chat starts with the context, whichever model you pick.
  • Your plan. It is a document you own. Check it against your numbers each month.
  • Refresh it every few months. When your business changes, run the interview again or ask Claude to update the instructions.
