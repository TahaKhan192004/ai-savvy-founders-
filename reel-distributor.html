The short answer

The Reel Distributor is a system that collects your Instagram reels, rewrites each caption for the platform it is going to, and posts the video to YouTube Shorts, TikTok, LinkedIn and more.

It runs on your own accounts, takes about 5 minutes per reel, and never posts without your click.

01

The problem it solves

You record a great reel, post it on Instagram, and stop there.

  1. Download the video from Instagram.
  2. Rewrite the caption for every platform.
  3. Log in to each app and upload.
  4. Try to remember what you already posted.

Done by hand, that takes 30 to 45 minutes per reel, so it does not get done. With this system it takes a couple of minutes, and you stay in control of what goes live.

02

The workflow at a glance

Six stages take a reel from Instagram to every platform. You do two of them. The system does the rest.

StageWhat happensWho does it
1. CollectYour latest Instagram reels are pulled in with their views, likes and comments.System, on one button press
2. ChooseYou see all reels in one list, sorted by what performed best, and pick one.You
3. PrepareThe video is downloaded in a format every platform accepts, and the caption is rewritten in the style of each platform.System
4. ReviewYou read and edit each caption. Nothing posts without your click.You
5. PublishOne click posts to the platforms you ticked.System
6. TrackEach reel shows where it has been posted, so you never double-post or forget.System
Note

You can also add a reel by hand. Paste the link and caption if it is not in the list.

03

What the system is made of

Four parts work together, in plain English.

  1. A simple dashboard. You see your reels and press buttons here. It is the only part you ever touch.
  2. A reel list. It remembers every reel, its numbers, and where it has been posted. Think of it as a smart spreadsheet.
  3. A caption rewriter. It turns your Instagram caption into the right format for each platform: a YouTube title and description, a short TikTok caption or a LinkedIn post.
  4. Publishing connections. They post on your behalf, using accounts you own. Almost any major platform can be added.
Important

Videos are held in temporary storage only while posting and are deleted automatically after 24 hours.

05

What you need to bring

Everything runs on your own accounts, so you own and control it all.

  1. Your Instagram account, set to a public profile.
  2. Your accounts on each platform you want to post to.
  3. A few low-cost tool subscriptions: a database and storage service, a reel-collection service, and the posting services that connect to each platform. Many have free or low-cost plans to start.
  4. About a few cents per reel collection in usage fees. Day-to-day running costs stay low.

07

Your daily routine

About 5 minutes per reel.

  1. Open the dashboard and press Refresh list.
  2. Pick a reel that performed well and has not been posted everywhere yet.
  3. Press Publish, and wait a few seconds while the captions are written.
  4. Read the captions and tweak anything that does not sound like you.
  5. Choose the platforms and press Publish now.
  6. Check that each platform shows a green live message.
08

Good to know

Honest expectations before you start.

  • You approve everything.The system writes drafts. It posts only when you click.
  • YouTube may hold new videos as private at first.Google applies this to new, unreviewed connections until they pass a review. Posting still works, and we guide you through the review if you want public videos immediately.
  • Platform limits apply.Platforms such as YouTube and TikTok cap how many videos can be posted per day. If a platform is busy, the system shows a clear message and the other platforms still go through.
  • Connections occasionally need renewing.Platforms sometimes ask you to re-approve access. It takes about a minute, and we show you how.
  • Instagram stays the source.We collect only your own public reels, and never need your Instagram password.
  • Proven end to end.This workflow has been tested live across multiple platforms.
  • Captions are a starting point.The rewrite is good, not perfect, so a quick read before posting is part of the process.
Warning

If a platform asks you to re-approve access, do it the same day. Posts to that platform pause until the connection is renewed.

FAQ

Questions answered

What is the Reel Distributor?

It is a system that collects your Instagram reels, rewrites the caption for each platform, and posts the video to the platforms you choose, such as YouTube Shorts, TikTok and LinkedIn, while recording what has been posted where.

Does it post without my approval?

No. The system writes drafts and posts only when you click publish.

Do I need to share my Instagram password?

No. It collects only your own public reels and never needs your Instagram password.

How long does it take per reel?

About 5 minutes with the daily routine, compared with 30 to 45 minutes when reposting by hand.

What happens to the videos?

Videos are held in temporary storage only while posting and are deleted automatically after 24 hours.

Who owns the system?

You do. Everything runs on your own accounts, so you own and control it.

Why might YouTube hold my videos as private?

Google applies this to new, unreviewed connections until they pass a review. Posting still works, and the build includes guidance through the review if you want public videos immediately.
