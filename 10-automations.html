01

Reply to new leads while they’re still interested.

How it's done

Connect your form builder (like Typeform or Fillout) to Zapier. When a new submission arrives, trigger Claude to draft a personalized reply based on their answers, and immediately send it via Gmail to book a call.

02

Turn call notes into follow-up drafts.

How it's done

Use a meeting recorder like Fathom or Fireflies. When a meeting ends, a webhook sends the transcript to Claude. Claude extracts action items, drafts a professional follow-up email, and drops it straight into your Gmail Drafts folder for review.

03

Text back when you miss a call.

How it's done

Use GoHighLevel or Twilio. Set a workflow trigger for "Incoming Call Status: Missed." The system immediately sends a pre-written SMS (e.g., "Sorry I missed you! I'm on another line. How can I help?") so the prospect doesn't immediately call a competitor.

04

Send the freebie when someone comments your keyword.

How it's done

Connect ManyChat to your Instagram or Facebook. Set a trigger for when a user comments a specific word (like "DASHBOARD"). ManyChat automatically sends them a direct message with the link and adds them to your CRM.

05

Follow up with leads who went quiet.

How it's done

Set a time-based trigger in your CRM (e.g., "Status unchanged for 14 days"). This triggers an automation to send a polite, plain-text email asking if they are still looking for help with their project, reopening the conversation.

06

Publish your reels across platforms.

How it's done

When you post a Reel on Instagram, a tool like Repurpose.io or Make automatically downloads the video without the watermark. Claude rewrites the caption to fit the new platform's style, and it's scheduled to TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and LinkedIn.

07

Ask for a review when a project wraps.

How it's done

Moving a client card to "Done" in Asana or Trello triggers a 3-day delay in Zapier. After 3 days, it automatically sends an email thanking them for their business and providing a direct link to your Google Business or Trustpilot page.

08

Chase overdue invoices with a polite reminder.

How it's done

Connect QuickBooks, Stripe, or Xero to your email automation. If an invoice passes its due date by 3 days, it triggers a gentle follow-up email. At 7 and 14 days, Claude can escalate the tone of the email slightly to ensure prompt payment.

09

Pull the day’s numbers into one sheet.

How it's done

Run a daily Zap at 11 PM that pulls your ad spend from Meta, traffic metrics from Google Analytics, and new sales from Stripe. It takes these metrics and appends them as a new row in a Google Sheet, creating an automated daily tracker.

10

Prepare a brief before each meeting.

How it's done

A morning automation checks your Google Calendar. For each external attendee, it uses an API to scrape their LinkedIn profile or company website. Claude reads the data, summarizes it into a 1-page briefing, and sends it to you in Slack 15 minutes before the call.
