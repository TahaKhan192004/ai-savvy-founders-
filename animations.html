10 Claude animation styles: a prompt pack

These are 10 general animation styles, not one scene. Each prompt works for any subject: a product, a character, a logo, an object or an idea. Replace [SUBJECT] with what you want animated, paste it into Claude, and you get a looping animation as a single HTML file in a 9:16 vertical frame (1080×1920).

The reel uses a cyclist only as a demo subject. It shows 10 styles in about 14 seconds, roughly 1.2 seconds each.

Fill in one thing

Replace [SUBJECT] with the thing you want animated and what it does, for example "a coffee cup with rising steam", "a rocket lifting off", "a person typing on a laptop", "my logo assembling itself" or "a plant growing".

Workflow

  1. Paste the base spec, then the style prompt, as one message. Each style prompt also works on its own.
  2. Ask Claude to make it an artifact so it plays in the browser.
  3. Check the loop: pedals, wheels and background must stay in sync with no visible jump.
  4. Screen-record 3–5 seconds per style at 1080×1920 and cut ~1.2 s from each.

Base spec

Prepend this to any style

This describes your subject and the motion requirements shared by every style. Replace the bracketed subject, paste it first, then add one style prompt below it.

Base spec
Create a single self-contained HTML file (inline CSS + JS, no external libraries or images) with a looping animation in a 9:16 vertical frame, 1080x1920, scaled to fit the viewport and centred.

Subject: [SUBJECT AND WHAT IT DOES, e.g. 'a coffee cup with rising steam', 'a rocket lifting off', 'a person typing on a laptop', 'my logo assembling itself', 'a plant growing'].

Requirements:
- The subject is the clear focal point, large and near the centre.
- Give it a primary motion (its main action) plus secondary motion (idle bob, blinking, particles, cloth or steam).
- Add depth with 2-3 background layers moving at different speeds (parallax) or subtle ambient motion.
- Seamless 4-6 second loop with no visible jump.
- Draw everything with SVG or canvas; smooth 60fps with requestAnimationFrame.
- No UI controls; add text only if the style calls for it.

Styles 1 to 5

Retro, chalk, neon, console and comic

Style 1 of 10

Pixel Art

Retro 8-bit game screen: chunky pixels, a limited palette and a game HUD.

Style prompt
Animate [SUBJECT] in 8-bit / 16-bit retro pixel art, like a classic side-scrolling game.
- Render on a low-res canvas (about 135x240) and scale up with image-rendering: pixelated so every pixel is a crisp square.
- Limited palette of about 12 colours; gradients only as 3-4 hard colour bands.
- Background: a pixel environment that suits the subject (city, forest, space, room) with tiny twinkling or flickering pixel details.
- Animate the subject as a sprite with 4-6 stepped frames at about 12fps, while the background scrolls smoothly.
- Add a game HUD in a pixel font: a title or score at the top, a counter that ticks up, and pixel hearts or an icon.
- Optional: a coin, power-up or 'LEVEL UP' pop to sell the game feel.
02

Style 2 of 10

Chalkboard Style

A hand-drawn chalk lesson: white stick figure on green slate inside a wooden frame.

Style prompt
Animate [SUBJECT] as a hand-drawn chalk lesson on a classroom chalkboard.
- Background: dark green board (#2f5a3c) with chalk-dust smudges and noise texture, inside a thick brown wooden frame.
- All line work in off-white chalk: rough, slightly wobbly strokes (SVG turbulence/displacement filter or jittered paths) with uneven opacity.
- Draw the subject as a simple chalk sketch with stick-figure or diagram-level detail.
- Title at the top in a handwritten font (e.g. '[TOPIC] 101'), a short formula or note in yellow chalk with a green tick, and an arrow doodle labelling the action.
- Lines and text draw themselves on (stroke-dashoffset) during the first second, then the subject animates.
- Add motion lines, small labels and a tiny stick figure for scale.
03

Style 3 of 10

Glow Sketch

Gold neon outline drawing under a stage spotlight on pure black.

Style prompt
Animate [SUBJECT] as a glowing gold neon line sketch on a black stage.
- Background pure black (#000) with a soft cone-shaped spotlight beam from the top centre (warm white at about 8% opacity).
- Draw the subject only as outlines: warm gold (#f2c14e) strokes 3-4px, no fills.
- Glow: stack 2-3 blur/drop-shadow layers in amber so lines look like neon tubes, with a gentle flicker.
- A glowing elliptical ring on the floor under the subject like a stage mark.
- Optional small spaced-out caps label at the top (e.g. 'T I T L E') in dim gold.
- Intro: lines trace on one by one, then the glow switches on as the background fades from grey to black.
04

Style 4 of 10

PS2 Style

Early-2000s console 3D: low-poly, flat-shaded faces, dusk lighting.

Style prompt
Animate [SUBJECT] in early-2000s PlayStation 2 era low-poly 3D style, faked in 2D.
- Build the subject from flat-shaded polygons: each form has 2-3 facets in a light and a dark shade to suggest hard lighting; no smooth gradients on the subject.
- Slightly blocky proportions, simple textures, limited colour banding.
- Background: a low-poly environment with a dusk gradient sky (lavender to peach) and haze on the far layer.
- Add subtle vertex wobble (1px jitter on polygon points) for the retro console feel.
- Optional: a slow orbiting camera move or a game-style title card.
05

Style 5 of 10

Comic Art

Pop-art comic page with halftone dots, sound effects and a reaction panel.

Style prompt
Animate [SUBJECT] as a pop-art comic book page.
- Layout: 2-3 panels with thick black borders and white gutters; the main action panel takes about 65% of the height.
- Thick black ink outlines and cel shading on everything; halftone dot patterns in skies and shadows.
- A starburst with a sound effect suited to the action (e.g. 'ZOOM!', 'POW!', 'BOOM!') that pops in with a scale bounce and shakes.
- Speed or action lines around the subject.
- A reaction panel: a small cartoon face with wide eyes and a speech bubble ('WOW!').
- Panels slightly tilted with drop shadows, as if printed on paper.

Styles 6 to 10

Flat, minimal, cinematic, brick and 3D

06

Style 6 of 10

Flat Vector

Clean modern flat illustration, the kind used in explainer videos.

Style prompt
Animate [SUBJECT] as a clean flat vector illustration: solid fills only, no outlines, no textures.
- Geometric, rounded shapes with a cohesive 5-6 colour palette (e.g. navy, cream, mustard, coral, sky blue).
- Simple layered background that suits the subject, with one or two bold focal shapes (a big moon, sun, circle or blob).
- Smooth motion-graphics animation: ease-in-out on every move, gentle squash and stretch, staggered timing between elements.
- Optional: shapes morph or slide in to build the scene in the first second.
07

Style 7 of 10

Dan Koe Style

Minimal, philosophical black-and-white line art in the look popular with self-improvement creators.

Style prompt
Animate [SUBJECT] as ultra-minimal monochrome line art on black, calm and philosophical.
- Background pure black; 3-4 faint concentric circles (white at 6-10% opacity, 1px) behind the subject that slowly pulse or rotate.
- Subject drawn in thin white strokes only (1.5-2px), no fills, simplified to geometric essentials.
- One thin horizontal ground line; a tiny stick figure or small element for scale and contrast.
- Optional rows of small white dots or particles drifting slowly.
- Very slow, deliberate motion with lots of negative space.
- Optional: one short line of white text fades in, e.g. a single idea in lowercase.
08

Style 8 of 10

CGI Motion

Cinematic night render: soft lighting, a glowing bike and a wet street reflection.

Style prompt
Animate [SUBJECT] in a cinematic CGI motion-graphic style.
- Moody dark background (#141c26) with a blurred environment for depth of field and slow parallax.
- Give the subject volume with soft radial gradients, specular highlights and rim lighting; no flat fills.
- Add a coloured glow on key parts and motion blur on fast-moving elements.
- A glossy reflective floor: a mirrored copy of the subject below at about 35% opacity, blurred and fading out.
- Light streaks, floating dust particles and a soft vignette.
- Overall feel: premium, like a 3D product render or a film title sequence.
09

Style 9 of 10

Lego Style

Everything is built from toy bricks, including the subject.

Style prompt
Animate [SUBJECT] as a toy-brick build, like a stop-motion brick animation.
- Build the subject and scene from small bricks on a grid; each brick has a highlight edge, a shadow edge and a round stud on top.
- Bright primary colours: red, yellow, blue, green, orange, white.
- Background: sky blue with brick-shaped clouds; brick stacks or brick scenery around the subject; a green studded baseplate as the ground.
- Animate in stepped, slightly jerky frames (8-10fps) to mimic stop-motion.
- Optional intro: bricks drop in and snap together to build the subject.
10

Style 10 of 10

ZackD Films

Soft 3D character render on a warm, blurry studio backdrop, like short explainer animations.

Style prompt
Animate [SUBJECT] in a soft 3D-render explainer style, warm and cinematic.
- Background: warm brown-to-tan radial gradient (#c79a72 centre to #5a3a24 edges) with large soft bokeh circles drifting, plus a vignette.
- Smooth rounded shapes with gentle gradients to suggest 3D; soft shadows where forms overlap; no outlines.
- Characters (if any) have simple rounded features and matte, clay-like skin and clothing.
- A soft elliptical contact shadow under the subject.
- Close, shallow-focus camera: keep the subject large in frame; add a slow push-in.
- Weighty, eased motion, like a short educational 'what happens if' film.

Building the full reel

The reel wrapper

The reel itself is a white backdrop, a title, a numbered list that fills in, and a tilted 3D card that swaps style every 1.2 seconds, ending on a "Comment Claude" grid. You can have Claude build that wrapper too.

Reel wrapper prompt
Create a single self-contained HTML file, 1080x1920 (9:16), that plays a 14-second showcase reel and then loops.

Layout:
- Light grey-white background (#efefef) with a soft vignette.
- Title at the top centre: '10' in bold black, then 'Claude' in a red-maroon italic serif with a small asterisk/sparkle mark, next line 'Animation styles' in bold black, and below it '(To Go Viral)' in small grey text.
- Left side: a numbered list 1) to 10). Each item's style name appears (typed or faded in) at the moment its style is shown; earlier names stay visible.
- Right side: a large rounded-corner card, about 45% of the width, tilted in 3D (CSS perspective, rotateY about -12deg) with a soft drop shadow. The card contains the current style's animation.

Sequence (about 1.2s each): Pixel Art, ChalkBoard Style, Glow Sketch, PS2 Style, Comic Art, Flat Vector, Dan Koe Style, CGI Motion, Lego Style, ZackD Films. Use a quick cut or a 150ms crossfade between styles. [Use the same [SUBJECT] in all 10 styles from this pack as the card contents.]

Outro (last ~2s): the background turns near-black, the list fades out, and a 3x3 grid of small cards from the styles appears with 'Comment “Claude” for the Prompts' centred in a white serif, 'Claude' in the red-maroon italic.

The bracketed line tells Claude to use the same subject in all ten styles. Replace [SUBJECT] with yours, or describe a different subject for each style.

Troubleshooting

Fixes when a result is off

ProblemFollow-up prompt
Style feels generic"Push the style harder: exaggerate its signature traits and remove anything from other styles."
Loop visibly jumps"Make each background layer a tile exactly as wide as its repeat distance and wrap the offset with modulo."
Subject moves stiffly"Add easing, overlap and follow-through so parts move slightly after the main motion."
Scene feels empty"Add secondary motion: particles, ambient background movement and a subtle camera drift."
Choppy playback"Use requestAnimationFrame with delta time and transform-only animation; avoid re-creating DOM nodes each frame."
Wrong framing"Keep the subject at about 40% of frame height, centred horizontally, ground at 70% of frame height."

Export tips

  • Open the HTML full-screen in Chrome and record with OBS or your phone's screen recorder at 1080×1920, 30 or 60 fps.
  • Record 3–5 s per style, then trim the cleanest 1.2 s loop in CapCut or Premiere.
  • Ask Claude to add ?style=3 URL switching to one file so you can record all ten from one page.
  • Keep one subject across all 10 styles for a reel like this, or pick a different subject for each style.
