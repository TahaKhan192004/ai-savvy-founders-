What you're getting

A skill is a set of instructions Claude follows the same way every time. This pack is 13 of them, each one doing a single Instagram job, like a small content team that already knows your voice.

  1. Install the pack.
  2. Teach it your voice.
  3. Ask for a reel, caption or plan.
  4. Check it and post it yourself.

Who made it

The Instagram agent skill was built by Jake Schincariol and released for free under the MIT licence. All the credit for the skills goes to him. You'll find the original at:

GitHub repositorygithub.com/Jakeschincariol/instagram-agent-skill

The honest part

  • It writes, you post. None of the skills log in to Instagram or post for you. Every skill ends with a copy-ready draft.
  • Your account stays safe. No passwords, no bots, no auto-following. Those are the things that get accounts blocked.
  • It never makes things up. If a draft needs a number you haven't given it, you'll see {{your number}} instead of an invented one.

Part 1 · Setup

Install the pack

  1. Get the Claude app. Download it from claude.ai/download for Mac or Windows and sign in. A paid plan gives you the most room to use the skills.
  2. Open the Code tab. At the top of the app, click Code. This is the part of Claude that can install skills on your computer. You won't need to write any code.
  3. Paste the link and ask Claude to install it. Copy the prompt below. Claude will ask before it does anything on your computer. Read what it says, then approve.
Type this
https://github.com/Jakeschincariol/instagram-agent-skill

Install this skill, then confirm /ig-reel works.
  1. Check it worked. Type the prompt below. You should see 13 skills that start with ig-.
Type this
What Instagram skills do you have now?
If it gets stuck
If something stops halfway, type: "That didn't finish. Explain what went wrong in plain language and fix it."
02

Part 1 · Setup

Teach it your voice

Every skill reads one file called voice.md. It holds who you are, who you talk to and how you actually speak. Skip this and everything sounds generic. Spend ten minutes here and the drafts sound like you on camera.

The fast way

Paste three of your best reel scripts or captions into Claude, then type:

Type this
Write my voice.md for the Instagram skills from these. Ask me anything you need to know about my audience, my offer and the words I'd never say. Save it where the skills can read it.

What it will ask you about

Who you are

What you do in one sentence, who you talk to, what you sell.

How you sound

Calm or loud, emoji or not, words you use and words you'd never say.

What you believe

Three to five opinions some of your audience disagrees with. Great reels come from these.

Proof you can use

Real numbers and stories you're happy to put your name to.

Good to know
Be specific about your audience. "Coaches charging under $2,000" gets far better drafts than "entrepreneurs".

Your 13 skills

Type the command, then what you want. The example in each row is one you can copy.

SkillWhat it doesExample
01 · /ig-reelTurns one idea into a reel. Gives you 3 hooks scored out of 100, then the script, the on-screen text and the timing./ig-reel why most coaches post every day and still don't grow
02 · /ig-viralFinds the reels doing best in your niche and ranks them by how far each one beat its account's normal views./ig-viral find what's working for business coaches this week
03 · /ig-captionWrites your caption and shows you the first line people see before they tap "more"./ig-caption for this reel script: [paste]
04 · /ig-carouselWrites a swipe post: the cover, every slide and the slide images./ig-carousel 5 mistakes people make with their first offer
05 · /ig-storyPlans your daily stories and which sticker to use for each one./ig-story plan today's stories to promote my free guide
06 · /ig-profileScores your profile out of 100 and rewrites what's losing points./ig-profile here's my bio and highlights: [paste]
07 · /ig-planPlans your week: what to post, when, and 10 accounts to engage with./ig-plan my week. I can film 3 reels on Monday.
08 · /ig-humanMakes any draft sound human. Takes out em dashes and AI words, then scores it./ig-human make this sound like me: [paste]
09 · /ig-commentWrites thoughtful comments on other people's posts. Never just emojis./ig-comment on this post: [paste the caption]
10 · /ig-replySorts the comments under your post and drafts your replies, leads first./ig-reply to these comments: [paste]
11 · /ig-dmWrites your keyword DMs, collab pitches and follow-ups./ig-dm the message for people who comment GUIDE
12 · /ig-repurposeTurns one video, podcast or newsletter into a week of reels and carousels./ig-repurpose this YouTube transcript: [paste]
13 · /ig-auditLooks at what you've already posted and shows what's actually working./ig-audit my last 10 reels: [paste views and saves]
Good to know
No need to remember the commands. You can also ask in normal words, like "use the reel skill to turn this idea into a script".

Part 3 · Use it

A week on autopilot

Here's a simple rhythm. Move it around to fit your week.

DayDo thisDetails
Monday · Plan/ig-plan my weekThen /ig-reel for each idea it gives you.
Tuesday · FilmFilm the reelsRead the scripts out loud. If a line feels wrong to say, ask for a new one.
Wednesday · Write/ig-caption and /ig-humanOne caption per reel, cleaned so it sounds like you.
Thursday · Swipe/ig-carouselTurn your best reel idea into a carousel too.
Every day · Engage/ig-reply and /ig-comment15 minutes on your comments and 5 other accounts.
Friday · Learn/ig-audit and /ig-viralSee what worked, and what's working for others.
Start small
Start with just /ig-plan and /ig-reel in week one. Add one more skill each week.

Prompt cheat sheet

When you wantType this
Better hooksGive me 5 more hooks for this. Make them more specific and less salesy.
Shorter scriptCut this script to 20 seconds. Keep the hook and the last line.
Sounds like AI/ig-human this, then rewrite anything it flags.
Fix my bio/ig-profile and fix the top 3 things first.
One idea, more posts/ig-repurpose this reel into 2 carousels and 3 stories.
New CTAChange the call to action to: comment GUIDE for the free guide.
Content ideasWhat are 10 reel ideas my audience would save, based on my voice.md?

Before you post

  • The hook is on screen in the first second.
  • You'd actually say every line out loud.
  • Any number in it is real and yours.
  • One clear ask at the end: save, comment or follow.
  • Five hashtags or fewer.
