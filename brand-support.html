Make your brand rescue a tipping truck in Google Flow

One copy-paste prompt and five steps to a 6-second vertical dashcam video with your brand on the truck.

Open Google Flow, set it to 9:16 with Veo 3.1, paste the prompt below with your brand details filled in, generate four takes and keep the one where the brand text stays correct. You need Google Flow and nothing else. No reference image is required.

1

Fill in your brand details

The prompt has five blanks. Write your answers in a note so you can paste them in.

BlankWhat to enterExample
[BRAND NAME]Brand name in capitals.AI SAVVY FOUNDERS
[LOGO]A short description of your logo.maroon geometric AI monogram
[BRAND COLOR]Hex code for the logo.#7A1F2B
[TEXT COLOR]Hex code for the brand name lettering.#271512
[SKYLINE]The city backdrop behind the barrier.a tall glass tower and a hazy modern skyline
2

Set up Google Flow

Set the format first so every take comes out vertical.

  1. Open Google Flow from Google Labs and sign in with your Google account.

  2. Click + New project and name it after your brand.

  3. In the generation settings, set the aspect ratio to Portrait 9:16.

  4. Choose the Veo 3.1 model.

  5. Set outputs per prompt to 4.

  6. Set the clip length to 6 seconds if the option is shown. If it is not, generate at 8 seconds and trim to 6.

Note

Veo 3.1 generates at 24 fps, so the prompt has no frame rate line.

3

Paste the prompt and negative prompt

Copy the prompt, swap in your five blanks, then add the negative prompt.

  1. In the Flow prompt bar, choose Text to Video.

  2. Click Copy on the prompt below and paste it into the prompt bar.

    Prompt
    Vertical 9:16 video, 6 seconds, photorealistic, shot as a real dashcam
recording from inside a car on a multi-lane city highway. Fixed
windshield-level camera, only a strip of the car bonnet visible at the
bottom of the frame. Overcast, dramatic cloudy sky, soft diffused
daylight, slightly muted colours, natural dashcam compression and mild
motion blur. [SKYLINE] sits in the distance behind a concrete barrier.
A beige taxi is in the left lane.

SUBJECT: A white/cream box truck with a canvas-covered cargo bed printed
with "YOUR BUSINESS" in bold dark type is tipping dangerously over on
its two left wheels, tilted about 45 degrees, driving ahead of the
camera and about to fall.

ACTION (timed):
0.0-1.8s: The tilted white truck drifts ahead, wobbling, wheels barely
holding the road. Traffic flows normally around it.
1.8-2.6s: A large dark grey box truck appears at the right edge of
frame, overtaking in the next lane. We see its LEFT SIDE PANEL first,
showing the [LOGO] and the name "[BRAND NAME]" on the panel, then its
rear doors as it pulls ahead.
2.6-3.6s: The grey truck steadies beside the tilting white truck and
pushes it upright with its side.
3.6-4.4s: The white truck rocks back onto all four wheels and
stabilises.
4.4-6.0s: Both trucks drive side by side away from the camera. The
camera holds steady and settles on the grey truck's REAR DOORS,
centered and clearly readable.

BRANDING (critical): On the grey truck's rear double doors, centered
horizontally, print the [LOGO] in [BRAND COLOR], about 45% of the door
width, with the text "[BRAND NAME]" in two lines below it. Line 1 is
larger, line 2 is smaller, in wide-tracked uppercase geometric
sans-serif, in [TEXT COLOR]. The graphic sits flat on the door panel,
following its perspective, with the door seams and hinges visible
through it like real vinyl. The same logo appears smaller on the
truck's side panel. The logo and text stay perfectly stable, sharp and
unchanged for the whole video, and they never morph, flicker or change
spelling.

Truck detail: matte dark grey paint, slightly dusty, red tail lights,
generic number plate, metal door hinges and vertical locking bars.

AUDIO: ambient highway road noise, wind, a heavy diesel engine passing,
tyre rumble. No music, no voiceover.

  3. Replace each blank in square brackets with your answers from Step 1.

  4. Add the negative prompt. If Flow has no negative prompt field, paste it at the end of the prompt on a new line that starts with Avoid:

    Negative prompt
    no text morphing, no misspelled or garbled lettering, no extra logos,
no watermark, no cartoon or CGI look, no camera shake, no zoom, no
cuts, no people visible, no crash or fire, no debris, no license plate
blur artifacts, no warped or melting trucks, no duplicated trucks, no
flickering graphics
4

Generate and pick the best take

Flow gives you four takes. Keep the one that passes the checklist.

  1. Click generate and wait for the four takes.

  2. Play each take and check it against the list below.

  3. If none pass, click generate again. Each run gives you four fresh takes.

  4. Download the best take at the highest resolution Flow offers.

One continuous shot, 9:16, no cuts, no camera shake.
The grey truck pushes the white truck upright with no crash or debris.
The brand name is spelled correctly on the rear doors.
The logo and text stay sharp and unchanged for the last 2 seconds.
Warning

Small lettering is the weak spot of video models. Pause on a few different frames and confirm the text looks the same in each before you approve a take.

5

Add the tagline (optional)

A tagline lands best in the last second, after the trucks settle.

  1. Open your downloaded video in any editor.

  2. Add your tagline as a text overlay from 5.0 to 6.0 seconds, centered in the lower third.

  3. Export at 9:16 and upload.

Tip

Keep the tagline short and clear of the truck's rear doors so it does not cover your logo.

FAQs

Do I need to upload my logo to Google Flow?

No. This SOP describes the logo in words inside the prompt. No reference image is needed.

Why is the brand text sometimes misspelled in the video?

Video models can garble small lettering. Generate four takes, keep the one where the text stays correct, and run the prompt again if none pass.

How long can a Google Flow clip be?

Veo 3.1 generates clips of 4, 6 or 8 seconds, so a 6-second clip is available.
