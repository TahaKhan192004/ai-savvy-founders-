Connect Meta Ads and Higgsfield to Claude

A step-by-step procedure to link your ad account and your ad creator to Claude, with no coding and no extra tools.

  11. 10Quick recap

Short answer: In claude.ai, go to Customize, then Connectors, and add a custom connector named Meta Ads with the link https://mcp.facebook.com/ads. Click Connect, log in to Facebook, and ask Claude about your ads. For ad creation, add Higgsfield the same way with https://mcp.higgsfield.ai/mcp.

Purpose

What you will be able to do

After this SOP, your Claude account can read your Meta ad data and create ads through Higgsfield. This is who it is for: founders and marketers who run Meta ads and want answers without opening Ads Manager.

  1. Ask Claude how your ads did this week and get the numbers straight from your account
  2. Spot what is working and what is wasting money, without opening Ads Manager
  3. Have Higgsfield create ad concepts for your product
  4. Have Claude turn the best ones into a Meta campaign, ready for you to review

Prerequisites

Before you start

Tick off these 3 items. Do this on a laptop or desktop at claude.ai. It is much easier than on a phone.

A Claude account. Free works for one connector. If you want both Meta Ads and Higgsfield, you need a paid plan (Pro or Max), because free accounts can only add one custom connector.
A Facebook login that can access your ad account. If someone else runs your Business Manager, ask them to give you access to the ad account first.
A Higgsfield account (only needed for Step 5). It uses Higgsfield credits to make ads.
01

Get the Meta Ads link

You need one link. Here it is, so you can skip the search if you like.

Meta Ads connector linkhttps://mcp.facebook.com/ads

If you would rather get it from Meta yourself:

  1. Go to Google and search Facebook Ads MCP
  2. Open the first result. It is Meta's own page, called Ads MCP Server | Meta for Developers
  3. Scroll down a little to the line that says the server is remote-hosted at https://mcp.facebook.com/ads
  4. Copy that link
02

Add Meta Ads to Claude

  1. Open claude.ai and log in
  2. In the left sidebar, click Customize
  3. At the top, click the Connectors tab
  4. Click the black + Add button on the right
  5. Choose Add custom connector
  6. A box pops up. In the first field (Name), type Meta Ads
  7. In the second field (URL), paste https://mcp.facebook.com/ads
  8. Click Continue
  9. Claude checks the link for a few seconds. On the next screen, leave all the options as they are and click Add

Meta Ads now shows in your connectors list.

03

Sign in to Facebook

You only do this once.

  1. After you click Add, you land on the Meta Ads connector page
  2. Click Connect
  3. A Facebook window opens. Log in with the account that has access to your ads
  4. Choose the business and ad account you want Claude to see, then approve
  5. You return to Claude, and it says you are connected
Note

Did not see the Connect button? Go to Customize, then Connectors, click Meta Ads in your list, and click Connect from there.

04

Test the connection

  1. Open a new chat in Claude
  2. Paste this prompt:
    Prompt
    What happened in my ads this week that I might have missed?
  3. The first time, Claude asks permission to use the Meta Ads connector. Click Allow
  4. Check the reply. You should see your real spend, your results, your best ad, your worst ad and what to do next

More prompts to try

Prompt
Which ad has the best return this month, and which one should I turn off?
Prompt
Compare this week to last week. What went up, what went down, and why?
Prompt
Which audience is my cost per result lowest on?
Prompt
Give me a 5-line summary of my ads I can read in 30 seconds every Monday.
05

Add Higgsfield

Optional, but this is where it gets fun. The steps are the same as Meta, with a different link.

  1. In Claude, go to Customize, then Connectors
  2. Click + Add, then Add custom connector
  3. For Name, type Higgsfield
  4. For URL, paste the link below Higgsfield connector linkhttps://mcp.higgsfield.ai/mcp
  5. Click Continue, leave the options as they are, and click Add
  6. Click Connect and log in to your Higgsfield account

Claude can now use both tools in the same chat.

06

Create ads with Higgsfield, build the campaign with Claude

6.1 Give Claude your product

  1. Upload a photo of your product, or paste the product page link
  2. Paste this prompt:
    Prompt
    This is my product. Create 10 ad concepts for it in Higgsfield.
Mix the styles: UGC, before and after, close-up, lifestyle and an offer ad.
Show me all 10 when they're done.

6.2 Pick the best ones

  1. Paste this prompt:
    Prompt
    Which 3 of these are most likely to perform well on Instagram and Facebook, and why?

6.3 Turn them into a Meta campaign

  1. Replace the budget placeholder with your own daily budget, then paste the prompt:
    Prompt
    Turn the best 3 into a new Meta campaign.
Objective: sales. Budget: [your daily budget].
Create it as PAUSED so nothing goes live until I check it.
Important

Keep the word PAUSED in the prompt. It is what stops the campaign from spending before you have looked at it.

6.4 Review before anything goes live

  1. Open Meta Ads Manager
  2. Find the new campaign
  3. Check the audience, the budget and the ads
  4. When you are happy, switch it on yourself

Safeguards

Keep your money safe

Warning

Always say "create it as paused" or "draft only" when you ask Claude to build or change a campaign. Then nothing spends until you have checked it.

Tip

Read what Claude is about to do. When Claude asks permission to run an action, read it before you click Allow.

Note

Higgsfield uses credits. Ten concepts use more credits than three, so start small.

Troubleshooting

If something goes wrong

FAQ

Common questions

Can I connect Meta Ads to Claude for free?

Yes. A free Claude account can add one custom connector, so you can connect Meta Ads. To add both Meta Ads and Higgsfield you need a paid plan such as Pro or Max.

What is the Meta Ads connector link for Claude?

The remote-hosted Meta Ads server link is https://mcp.facebook.com/ads. Paste it as the URL when you add a custom connector in Claude.

Will Claude spend my ad budget without asking?

Not if you ask for campaigns to be created as paused or as a draft only. Nothing spends until you review the campaign in Meta Ads Manager and switch it on yourself.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The whole setup happens in your browser at claude.ai and takes about 5 minutes for Meta Ads plus 2 minutes for Higgsfield.

Does Higgsfield cost anything to use through Claude?

It uses credits from your Higgsfield account to create ads. Start with a few concepts and scale up.

Summary

Quick recap

  1. Copy the link https://mcp.facebook.com/ads
  2. In Claude go to Customize, then Connectors, then + Add, then Add custom connector
  3. Name it Meta Ads, paste the link, click Continue, then Add
  4. Click Connect and log in to Facebook
  5. Ask: "What happened in my ads this week that I might have missed?"
  6. Optional: add Higgsfield with https://mcp.higgsfield.ai/mcp and let it make your ads
