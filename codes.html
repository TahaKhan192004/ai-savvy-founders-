7 codes to type before your prompts

Type the word, then ask your question. Each one turns Claude into a different specialist.

2 min read Beginner Updated August 2026

No prompt engineering course needed. These are plain words. Drop one in front of your question and Claude answers from a completely different angle.

01 80/20

Find what to stop doing

Ask which twenty percent of what you are doing is actually driving results. It tells you what to cut.

02 /ghost

Strip the AI fingerprint

Put this in front of your prompt and you get a humanized version, no AI slop, no giveaway phrasing.

03 artifacts

Build it instead of describe it

Say the word and Claude stops explaining the thing and starts building it. A working dashboard, a pricing calculator, a small app, live in the chat while you watch.

04 expose

Find the real question

Names the real question sitting underneath the one you asked. Uncomfortable. Also the fastest way to find out what you are actually stuck on.

05 skill critic

Get the honest answer

Claude stops being the yes man and gives you the answer you needed, not the one that feels good.

06 OODA

Decide faster

Runs a military decision framework over your problem. Observe, orient, decide, act.

07 L99

Go to expert depth

Tells Claude to answer at the highest expert level it can reach. Same question you were already asking, completely different depth coming back.

How to use these

  1. Open a new or existing Claude chat.
  2. Type the code word first, in your own message.
  3. Follow it directly with your real question.
  4. Read the response. If it is not sharp enough, add the code again and ask it to go further.
Note

These codes are plain language instructions, not hidden settings. They work in any Claude conversation, on any plan.
