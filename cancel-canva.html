Start here

Cancel Canva. Let Claude design your carousels.

Build a Claude Project that knows your brand, follows your design system, and designs every carousel slide for you. No templates, no Canva.

TimeAbout 20 minutes once, then 5 to 10 minutes per carousel
DifficultyBeginner
You needA Claude account, your brand's best past work, a website (if you have one)
Last updatedNovember 2026

How this works

The whole setup is three moves.

  1. Extract. Prompt 1 reads your website and your past work, then writes your brand design system as one file: brand-design.md.
  2. Save. You lock that file into a Claude Project, together with the carousel instructions (Prompt 3).
  3. Build. From then on, you give Claude an idea and it writes the copy, designs the slides in your brand, and exports them ready to post.

You do the extraction once. Every carousel after that is on brand without you explaining your colors, fonts or layout again.

Before you start

Gather these first

It takes five minutes and makes the extraction far sharper.

  1. Your website URL. Claude reads the live design from it.
  2. 5 to 10 images of your best past work. Carousel slides, social graphics, a slide deck, your logo, a brand board. Screenshots are fine.
  3. Your best single piece. Pick the one file that looks most like your brand. Claude treats it as the north star when two sources disagree.
  4. 2 to 3 designs you wish yours looked like (optional). Screenshots or links. This helps when your site has not caught up to the look you want.
  5. Settings check. In Claude, open Settings and make sure web search and file creation are switched on. Names can vary slightly by plan.

02

Step 2

Run Prompt 1 to extract your brand design

  1. Inside the project, click New chat.
  2. Copy the full prompt below.
  3. Replace the four bracketed lines in the INTAKE section with your own details.
  4. Send it.
Prompt 1: Extract your brand design
You are a senior brand designer. Your job is to reverse-engineer my complete design system from the work I have already made, so every future graphic can be built on-brand without me explaining it again.

Work in the order below. Do not skip steps. Do not invent anything. If you cannot confirm a value, say so.

INTAKE
First check what I have given you: the files in this project, anything attached to this message, and the details below. If something essential is missing, ask me for all of it in one message and wait for my answer.

Brand name (the name it goes to market under): [BRAND NAME]
Website: [WEBSITE URL, or "none"]
My best piece of work (the file that looks most like my brand): [FILE NAME, or "none"]
Designs I admire: [LINKS OR FILE NAMES, or "none"]

SOURCES
1. Website. Fetch the homepage and 2 to 3 inner pages. Read the visible design: colors, fonts, spacing, buttons, cards, section rhythm. If the site is built in a way you cannot read, tell me and ask for screenshots. Never guess values.
2. My files. Look at every image, PDF and deck in this project. For each one, note colors, type style, layout structure, whitespace, corner shapes, shadows, overlays, decorative elements and where the brand mark sits.
3. Carousels. If any of my files are carousel slides, identify the cover, body and closing slide patterns, the text zones, the slide number position and how the accent color is used.

CONFLICTS
- My best piece of work wins for the overall look and feel.
- The website wins for exact values: hex codes, fonts and sizes.
- My files win for layout patterns.
- If my current brand and my old work disagree, ask me which one is current.
- List every conflict you find.

OUTPUT
Create one file called brand-design.md with these sections:

1. Brand summary: name, 3 to 5 mood words, and one sentence describing the look and feel.
2. Colors: a table with hex code, semantic role (background, surface, text, muted text, accent, border, dark section) and a usage rule for each color. Add a gradient section if the brand uses gradients.
3. Contrast check: test every text-on-background pairing against WCAG AA (4.5 to 1 for body text). Flag every pair that fails.
4. Typography: heading font, body font, weights, and a scale (display, h1, h2, h3, body, caption, eyebrow, button) with size, line height, letter spacing and text case. Add a Google Fonts import line if the fonts are on Google Fonts. If they are not, name the closest Google Fonts match and label it a substitute.
5. Spacing: base unit, spacing scale, content max width, margins and corner radius scale.
6. Components: buttons, cards, tags, dividers, and any icon or number style, each with exact specs.
7. Visual direction: photography, icons, textures, overlays, shadows, and what the brand never looks like.
8. Carousel rules: slide size, safe margins, cover slide layout, 3 to 4 body slide layouts, closing slide layout, slide number and brand mark placement, and how the accent color is used.
9. CSS variables: a copy-ready :root block holding every color, font, spacing and radius value.
10. Do Not rules: 6 to 8 rules specific to this brand, based on what you saw.
11. Assumptions and gaps: everything you approximated or could not confirm.

Mark every value FOUND (read from the website or a file) or APPROXIMATED (estimated from an image).

When you finish, show me the full brand-design.md, then list the conflicts and gaps you need me to resolve. Ask them as a numbered list so I can answer by number.
  1. Wait for Claude to finish. It will give you brand-design.md plus a numbered list of questions.
  2. Answer the questions by number in your next message. You will paste those answers into Prompt 2.
If Claude cannot read your website

If Claude says your website could not be read, upload 3 to 5 screenshots of your homepage and key pages, then send:

Step 3

Run Prompt 2 to review and lock the design

Prompt 2 applies your answers, fixes contrast problems, and proves the design works by rendering a test slide before you save anything.

  1. In the same chat, copy the prompt below.
  2. Replace the bracket with your numbered answers.
  3. Send it.
Prompt 2: Review and lock
Here are my answers to your questions and conflicts:

[PASTE YOUR NUMBERED ANSWERS]

Now finalize brand-design.md.

1. Apply my answers.
2. Fix every failing contrast pair by changing only the text color. Never change a brand color to fix contrast. Tell me what you changed.
3. Replace every APPROXIMATED value that my answers now confirm. Keep the label on anything still approximated.
4. Build one HTML test page that shows: the color swatches, the type scale, a button, a card, and one example cover slide at 1080 by 1350 pixels. Use only values from the file. Show it to me.
5. Output the final brand-design.md as a downloadable file.

Do not change anything I did not ask you to change.

Open the test page Claude shows you. Check three things:

  1. The colors match your brand.
  2. The fonts look right.
  3. The example cover slide looks like something you would post.

If anything is off, tell Claude exactly what and ask it to fix that one thing, then ask for the final file again.

Important

Do not move to Step 4 until the test page looks like your brand. Everything you build later depends on this file.

04

Step 4

Save the brand design into your project

  1. Download the final brand-design.md from the chat.
  2. Go back to your project's main page.
  3. In the project knowledge panel, click + and upload brand-design.md.
  4. Delete any earlier draft of the file from project knowledge so Claude only sees the final one.
  5. Open the project instructions box and paste Prompt 3 below.
  6. Replace the three bracketed lines, then save.
Prompt 3: Carousel studio instructions
You are the carousel designer for [BRAND NAME]. You turn my ideas, posts and transcripts into on-brand carousels.

BRAND
Before every design, read brand-design.md in this project and follow it exactly: its colors, fonts, spacing, components and Do Not rules. Never invent or change a brand value. If brand-design.md is missing, stop and tell me.

MY VOICE
How I write: [2 TO 3 SENTENCES, for example "Direct and warm. Short sentences. Talks like a friend who has done the work."]
Words and patterns I never use: [YOUR LIST. Add "em dashes" if you dislike them.]

WORKFLOW
Follow these steps in order every time.

1. INTAKE. If I have not already told you, ask in one message: what the carousel is about, who it is for, what a reader should do after the last slide, how many slides (default 8), and the platform (default Instagram portrait, 1080 by 1350). If I paste long content, pull out the angle, audience and call to action yourself and confirm your read in two lines.

2. COPY FIRST. Write all the slide copy as plain text and wait for my yes before designing anything. Show it slide by slide.

3. DESIGN. Build one HTML file with one div class="slide" per slide. Put all styles and the font import in the head.

4. EXPORT. Render every slide to a numbered PNG (slide-01.png onward) at the exact export size. If I ask for LinkedIn, also create one combined PDF. If you cannot render images in this environment, tell me, give me the HTML, and give me step by step instructions to screenshot each slide at the export size.

5. CHECK. Before delivering, check every item in the CHECKLIST below and fix anything that fails.

SLIDE STRUCTURE (8 slides)
Slide 1: Hook. One idea that stops the scroll and promises a payoff.
Slide 2: Set up the problem the reader feels, or frame the promise.
Slides 3 to 6: Deliver. One point per slide. Never two ideas on one slide.
Slide 7: Payoff. The reframe or the summary.
Slide 8: Call to action. One action, stated plainly.
For other slide counts, keep the same order and stretch or trim the delivery slides.

COPY RULES
- Slide 1 is 6 to 10 words. If it needs more, the hook is unclear.
- Every other slide has a headline of 3 to 8 words and at most 25 words of support.
- Write at an 8th grade reading level. Short sentences. Active voice.
- No slide should make the reader zoom in.
- The last slide asks for exactly one thing.

LAYOUT RULES
- Every slide is exactly the export size with overflow hidden. Nothing runs off the edge.
- Keep an 80 pixel safe margin on all four sides.
- Body text is never smaller than 28 pixels at 1080 wide. Headlines run 60 to 140 pixels.
- Use the brand fonts from brand-design.md. Never swap a font without telling me.
- Vary the layout across slides using the layouts in brand-design.md. Eight identical slides look like a template.
- Add a small slide number in the same corner on every slide.
- Show the brand mark or handle on slide 1 and the call to action slide only.
- Use icons drawn as simple vector shapes. Never use emojis.

CHECKLIST
- Slide 1 is readable as a thumbnail about 150 pixels wide.
- Nothing is clipped and no text sits inside the safe margin.
- Body text is at least 28 pixels.
- Every slide uses the brand palette and fonts.
- No slide breaks a Do Not rule in brand-design.md.
- Layouts vary across the carousel.
- The last slide asks for exactly one thing.
- The slide count matches what we agreed.

When you deliver, tell me which brand file you applied and list any assumptions you made.

Then open a New chat inside the project and ask:

Check prompt
What brand are you designing for, and what are the first three colors in brand-design.md?

If Claude answers correctly, the setup is working.

Step 6

Use the edit bank

These are fixed one-line prompts for the fixes you will want most often. Paste them into the same chat as your carousel.

Weak hook

Prompt 5: Weak hook
Slide 1 is not stopping the scroll. Give me 5 new hooks of 10 words or fewer, each using a different angle. Wait for me to pick one, then rebuild slide 1.

Two ideas on one slide

Prompt 5: Two ideas on one slide
Slide [NUMBER] carries two ideas. Split it into two slides, keep every other slide the same, and renumber the carousel.

Repeating layouts

Prompt 5: Repeating layouts
Slides [NUMBER] and [NUMBER] use the same layout. Redesign slide [NUMBER] with a different layout from brand-design.md. Keep the copy exactly as it is.

Off-brand check

Prompt 5: Off-brand check
Re-read brand-design.md. For every slide, list each value that does not match it (color, font, size, spacing, Do Not rules), then fix them all.

New platform

Prompt 5: New platform
Resize this whole carousel to square 1080 by 1080 for LinkedIn, rebuild any slide that no longer fits, and also export one combined PDF.

New angle from the same idea

Prompt 5: New angle from the same idea
Write a second carousel from the same material with a different angle. Give me 3 angle options first and wait for my pick.

Teach the brand something new

Prompt 5: Teach the brand something new
Add this rule to brand-design.md: [YOUR RULE]. Output the complete updated file so I can replace the old one in project knowledge.

Where to post

Each format, in the right size

PlatformSizeUpload asNotes
Instagram1080 x 1350PNG images in orderPortrait gets the most feed space. The profile grid crops to a center square, so keep the key words of slide 1 near the middle. 6 to 10 slides works best.
LinkedIn1080 x 1080PDF documentAsk for the PDF export. Square is safer than portrait because some clients crop portrait.
Facebook1080 x 1080PNG imagesKeep copy tight. Text-heavy slides get less reach.

Troubleshooting

If something goes wrong

ProblemFix
Slides look off brandSend the off-brand check from the Edit Bank. If it keeps happening, confirm brand-design.md is listed in project knowledge.
Claude could not read your websiteUpload 3 to 5 screenshots and tell Claude to use them as the website source.
Text is clipped or crowdedAsk Claude to cut words. Do not let it shrink text below 28 pixels.
Wrong fonts appearOpen brand-design.md, section 4. If your font is not on Google Fonts, Claude used a labeled substitute. Upload the font file or pick a closer match.
Slides all look the sameSend the repeating layouts prompt, or ask Claude to use a different layout from section 8 of brand-design.md on every slide.
Claude cannot produce PNG filesAsk for the HTML, open it in your browser, and screenshot each slide at the export size. Claude gives you the steps.
You changed your brandRe-run Prompt 1 with your new files and website, then replace brand-design.md in project knowledge.

Optional level up

Turn your design into a reusable skill

Do this only if you use Claude Code. It turns brand-design.md into a skill, so Claude applies your brand to every visual task on your machine, not just inside this project.

  1. Open Claude Code in an empty folder and place your final brand-design.md in it.
  2. Name the skill after your brand, never after yourself. A founder called Joe Wicks whose brand is The Body Coach would use the-body-coach-design or tbc-design.
  3. Send Prompt 6.
  4. Restart Claude Code. Any carousel, graphic or page you ask for now follows your brand automatically.
Prompt 6: Brand design skill
Read brand-design.md in this folder. Turn it into a Claude Code skill named [BRAND-SLUG]-design and save it to ~/.claude/skills/[BRAND-SLUG]-design/.

Create two files:

1. SKILL.md with frontmatter (name and a description that triggers on any visual task for this brand: carousels, graphics, slides, landing pages, PDFs), then: mood words, color table with roles and usage, type scale, spacing scale, component specs, carousel rules, the copy-ready :root CSS block, the font import line, and the Do Not rules.
2. references/design-tokens.md with the complete detailed set: every hex code, every pixel value, every font spec, the contrast table, and component specs by state.

Do not change any value from brand-design.md. When done, tell me the exact path and remind me to restart Claude Code to load it.
