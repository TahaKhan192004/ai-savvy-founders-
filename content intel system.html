What You Are Building

A Claude Project that works as your full content team

One command and it handles research, ideation, writing, and repurposing across four platforms. Everything runs through your Voice skill, so output sounds like you wrote it.

Research Uses Apify to scrape real Instagram data from your account and favorite creators. Finds top-performing hooks, extracts audience language from comments, and identifies content gaps you can own.
Generate Ideas Content ideas backed by data, not guesses. Every idea traces back to a performing hook, a niche gap, or audience language.
Write for 4 Platforms Instagram (2,200 chars), LinkedIn (3,000 chars), Facebook (63,206 chars), Threads (500 chars). Each gets a different angle.
Draft Carousels and Reels Slide-by-slide carousel copy ready for design. Timed reel scripts with visual direction, ready to film.
Repurpose Turn one idea into all 4 platform posts plus a carousel and reel draft. Different angle per platform, not a copy-paste resize.
Write Hooks and Captions Scroll-stopping hooks organized by psychological angle. Platform-optimized captions with CTAs and character limits respected.
Before You Start

You need three things ready before the Project setup. If you have not built them yet, do them now.

0A

Install and Run the Voice Builder Skill

Your Voice skill is the multiplier. Every writing employee in this Project reads it before producing a single word. Without it, everything sounds like AI. With it, everything sounds like you.

You do not build it by hand. Download and install the Voice Builder Skill, then open your Business DNA Project and run it there. The skill handles everything automatically. It will ask you to upload sample content, extract your voice patterns, and generate your finished Voice skill for you. You do not need to follow a separate manual guide.

Important

Run this skill inside your Business DNA Project, not in a standalone conversation. This ensures your voice data stays connected to your business context.

0B

Verify Your ICP.md

Your ICP (Ideal Customer Profile) is the audience document your content team reads before writing anything. It tells every skill who you are talking to: their role, their pains, their desires, their objections, and how they talk about their problems.

You created your ICP.md when you set up your Business DNA Project. It should already be uploaded in your Project Knowledge. If you have not built it yet, follow the guide below before continuing.

0C

Connect Apify to Claude

Apify is the scraping engine that pulls real Instagram data into Claude. Without it, you are writing content from memory. With it, you are writing from data.

  1. Go to claude.ai and open any conversation.
  2. Click the plug icon (Connectors) in the bottom toolbar.
  3. Search for Apify and click Connect.
  4. Create a free Apify account at apify.com if you do not have one.
  5. Authorize Claude to use your Apify account.
  6. Done. Claude can now scrape Instagram for you inside any conversation.
Note

The free Apify tier gives you enough credits to run content intel on 3 to 4 handles (yours plus 2 to 3 creators). That is plenty for a monthly research cycle.

Setup
01

Open Your Business DNA Project

You already built this project during your Business DNA setup. You are not creating a separate project here. Every content intel skill, knowledge file, and instruction gets added to the same Business DNA Project you already have.

  1. Go to claude.ai and click Projects in the left sidebar.
  2. Open your existing Business DNA Project.
  3. You will see two sections: Custom Instructions and Knowledge. The following steps tell you exactly what goes where.

Keeping everything in one project makes the system smarter over time, because Claude learns from every conversation you have inside it.

Note

We recommend keeping everything in one project rather than creating separate projects for each workflow. One project means Claude has full context on your brand, audience, and voice every time you start a conversation.

02

Set Up Your Favorite Creators Sheet

Instead of a static file, you will use a Google Sheet called Favorite Creators. This is the list of accounts the Content Intel Agent researches when you say "run my content intel." Because it is a live sheet, you can update it anytime without re-uploading anything to your Project.

Create a Google Sheet called Favorite Creators. Add one tab per platform you want to research (start with Instagram).

In the Instagram tab, list one handle per row. That is it. No headers needed. Just the handles:

Instagram tab @yourhandle @competitor1 @competitor2 @competitor3

Put your own handle first, then 2 to 3 direct competitors, then any creators in adjacent niches whose content you want to study.

The system scrapes every handle it finds in the sheet. If you want smarter analysis (like separating competitor ideas from adjacent niche ideas), you can add a second column with a note like "competitor" or "adjacent," but it is optional. Handles alone work.

Connect this sheet to your Business DNA Project:

  1. Open your Business DNA Project.
  2. Go to the Knowledge section.
  3. Click the Google Drive icon and search for your Favorite Creators sheet.
  4. Select it. Done. The system reads it live, so any handle you add later is included in the next run automatically.
Tip

Do not limit this to direct competitors. Add creators whose content you admire, even if they are in a completely different niche. The system will pull hook patterns and content structures that you can adapt to your own space.

05

Test Your System

Open a new conversation inside your Project and run these tests to make sure everything works.

Test 1: Intel

Type: Run my content intel. It should read your Favorite Creators Google Sheet, use Apify to scrape the handles, and deliver a full report with hook bank, audience language, gap analysis, and 3 ideas.

Test 2: Repurpose

Take one idea from the intel report and type: Turn idea #1 into content. It should produce 4 platform posts plus a carousel draft and reel draft, all in your voice.

Test 3: Voice Check

Read every output out loud. Does it sound like you or does it sound like AI? If it sounds off, refine your Voice skill and run again.

Test 4: Character Limits

Check that Instagram posts are under 2,200 characters, LinkedIn under 3,000, and Threads under 500. If anything is over, the instructions need tightening. (They should not be, but verify.)

Reference

Commands Your System Supports

You do not need to memorize exact phrases. Say what you need and the system routes to the right skill.

Research

"Run my content intel" Scrapes your handles and favorite creators, delivers full intel report with hooks, audience language, gaps, and 3 ideas.
"What is working in my niche?" Same as above. Triggers the full research phase.
"Scrape @handle" Pulls recent posts from a specific account for analysis.

Content Creation

"Turn this into content" Takes any idea, transcript, or topic and produces all 4 platform posts plus carousel and reel drafts.
"Write me a reel about [topic]" Full timed script with spoken words, on-screen text, and visual direction.
"Write a carousel about [topic]" Slide-by-slide copy, cover to CTA.
"Write a caption for [platform]" Platform-optimized caption with CTA, within character limits.
"Write me hooks for [topic]" 20+ hooks organized by psychological angle.

Ideas

"Give me content ideas" Generates ideas backed by your latest intel report. If no intel exists, runs research first.
"What should I post this week?" Same as above, framed as a weekly plan.
"Give me 5 reel ideas" Ideas specifically formatted for reels, with hook directions.
"Give me 5 carousel ideas" Ideas specifically formatted for carousels, with slide count and angle.

Repurposing

"Repurpose this for all platforms" Adapts one piece for Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, and Threads. Each gets a different angle.
"Turn this reel into a carousel" Converts between formats.
"Turn this carousel into a LinkedIn post" Converts between formats.
Maintenance

Re-run every 30 days

Hooks stop working. Competitors shift. Your audience's language evolves. The system is designed to be re-run, not set and forgotten. Each new intel report replaces the old one and every downstream skill gets smarter.
