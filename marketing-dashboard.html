Why build this workflow?

If you have to manually gather data from Instagram, Meta Ads, Google Analytics, and your CRM just to figure out what is working, you won't do it. And if you aren't looking at your numbers, you are flying blind.

By using Composio to wire these platforms directly into Claude, your content performance and ad spend sit alongside your actual leads and revenue. Follow these five steps to turn disjointed metrics into a single, cohesive interface.

01

Connect Your Data Using Composio

Before Claude can build anything, it needs access to your actual numbers. Composio acts as the secure bridge between Claude and your marketing platforms.

Platform What it provides to Claude
Instagram & Facebook Page Content performance, reach, and engagement metrics.
YouTube View counts, watch time, and subscriber growth.
Meta Ads Ad spend, cost-per-click, and ROAS.
Google Analytics Traffic sources, bounce rates, and session durations.
Your CRM (HubSpot, GoHighLevel, etc.) Leads generated, tracked link sources, and total revenue.

How to do it

In your Claude interface, activate the Composio MCP or use the Composio plugin. Authorize your accounts one by one. Once authenticated, Claude has secure, read-only access to your live data.

Fails without

Active connections. If Composio is not properly authenticated, Claude will either hallucinate the numbers or return an error stating it cannot reach your data.

02

Set the Context in Claude

Now that the pipes are connected, you need to tell Claude who the dashboard is for and what you need to know. This grounds the AI before it starts pulling data.

Prompt 
I am building a marketing dashboard for [Client Name / Internal Team]. 
I need you to act as my data analyst.

Using Composio, verify your connection to my data sources: 
Instagram, YouTube, Facebook Page, Meta Ads, Google Analytics, and my CRM. 

Confirm you can read the latest top-level metrics from all six sources 
before we begin structuring the dashboard.
03

Generate the Core Dashboard

This is the main engine. Ask Claude to structure the raw data into a readable UI, ensuring you connect top-of-funnel metrics directly to your revenue.

Prompt 
Build me a marketing dashboard using my connected data. 

I need to see these five sections clearly visualized:
1. Traffic Sources (Google Analytics)
2. Content Performance (Instagram, YouTube, Facebook)
3. Ad Spend & ROAS (Meta Ads)
4. Leads Generated (CRM)
5. Total Revenue (CRM)

Crucially: Compare every metric this week with last week's numbers 
and show me the percentage change.

Why it works

It demands a rigid structure and forces a week-over-week percentage change, which is the only way to gauge actual momentum.

The Attribution Connection

Your tracked links and the source information in your CRM connect the dots. This step lets you see exactly where people found you, which sources brought in leads, and which became paying customers.

04

Make it Client-Ready

Takes the raw artifact Claude just built and applies your brand identity so you can present it professionally.

Prompt 
Update the dashboard UI to use my brand colours: 
Primary: [Hex Code]
Secondary: [Hex Code]

Add this logo URL: [Logo URL] to the header. 
Adjust the typography and spacing so it looks like a polished, 
client-ready report I would actually send to a stakeholder.
05

The Monday Summary

Once the dashboard is working, you have the numbers together to decide where to put your time and money. Run this prompt every Monday to dictate your week.

Prompt 
Based on these numbers, give me three specific things 
to focus my time and money on this week to improve revenue. 

Do not give me generic advice. Point directly to a working campaign 
to scale, or a failing channel to kill, based strictly on the data 
you just surfaced.
