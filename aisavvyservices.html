Questions

The things people
actually ask.

What is an AI employee?

An AI employee is a custom agent plus the automation around it that owns a repeatable job in your business end to end. Unlike a chatbot, it is not waiting for you to prompt it. It runs on a schedule or a trigger, touches your real tools, and produces an output you can act on.

What does an AI development company do?

An AI development company designs, builds and deploys AI systems into a business: agents that carry out multi-step work, chatbots grounded in your own documents, and automation that connects the tools you already use. We do that work and also build the websites, apps and software those systems run on, so one team covers the whole thing.

Do you offer web development as well as AI?

Yes. Web development, custom software, mobile apps, e-commerce and UI/UX design are core services, not an add-on. Most clients need both: an AI system is only useful once there is a site, app or dashboard where the work actually lands.

What technologies do you build with?

For AI: Claude, MCP, LangChain, LangGraph and Groq, with PgVector and Supabase for retrieval. For automation: n8n, Apify and GoHighLevel. For web and software: Next.js, React, React Native, TypeScript, FastAPI, Node.js, PostgreSQL, Supabase, Docker and Vercel.

Which service do I actually need?

You do not have to know. Bring the job that keeps landing back on your desk and we will tell you which of these services fits it, or whether it is better solved without automation at all. Most engagements start with one system and expand once it proves itself.

How long does a build take?

Most single systems ship in two to three weeks. Multi-system builds run four to six weeks. You get the delivery date in writing before we start building, and you see a working piece every week rather than one reveal at the end.

Do I need to be technical to work with you?

No. You need to know your business and be able to describe the job you want handled. We handle scoping, building, testing and handover. Every system ships with a walkthrough recording and written documentation so your team can run it without us.

Can you work with the tools we already use?

Yes, and that is the default. We build into your existing stack rather than asking you to migrate. The common integrations are GoHighLevel, Slack, Notion, Google Sheets, Supabase and Apify, but if a tool has an API it can be wired in.

Who owns the systems you build?

You do. Everything runs in your own accounts, on your own API keys, and ownership transfers at handover along with documentation and a recorded walkthrough. There is no platform to stay subscribed to and no lock-in.

Why Claude rather than another model?

Claude handles long context, tool use and multi-step reasoning well, and MCP lets it connect directly to the tools your business already runs on. That said, we are not religious about it. Lead scoring often runs on a fast, cheap model, and we pick per job rather than per fashion.
