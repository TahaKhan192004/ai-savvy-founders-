Questions

Quick answers before you start.

Can Claude replace Canva and Gamma?

Yes. Claude creates designs and slide decks in an editor built for each, applies your brand colors and fonts from a saved design system, and exports the result as PDF, image or PowerPoint.

How do Claude scheduled tasks replace Zapier?

You describe a workflow in plain English, connect the apps it touches and set a schedule. Claude then runs it at the time you choose, reading from and writing to apps such as Gmail, Notion and Google Drive.

Do I need Claude Code for all of these?

No. Nine of the twelve work in the regular Claude app. The video workflows that replace Opus Clip, Descript and CapCut use Claude Code because they run tools like Whisper, ffmpeg and Remotion.

What is an artifact in Claude?

An artifact is a page, document, design or small app that Claude builds and hosts for you. You can edit it, share it with a private link and ask Claude to change it at any time.
