Start here

Your fridge photo in the middle, a week of meals around it

Fridge Chef is a small app that reads photos of your fridge and pantry and turns what is inside into a full week of breakfast, lunch and dinner ideas (21 dishes), in the cuisine you choose.

Download the app file below, then ask Claude to publish it as your own private app using the prompt on this page. It takes a few minutes.

What the app does

  • At the top, you type a country and pick a cuisine: Local, Continental, American, Italian, Chinese and more.
  • The fridge sits in the center. Tap it or drop onto it to add 2 to 3 fridge or pantry photos. Claude lists the ingredients it sees.
  • In the "What's inside" list you can remove items or add ones Claude missed.
  • "Cook a week of meals" gives you 21 dishes: one breakfast, one lunch and one dinner for each of 7 days. Each needs at most 2 items you don't already have.
  • Day tabs (Mon to Sun) switch the day. That day's dishes appear as cards to the left of the fridge, each with a food icon, cooking time and red "+ item" tags for things to buy.
  • A "The week" panel on the right shows all seven days at a glance, plus a shopping list for the whole week.
  • Tap a card to open the full method and shopping list. "Copy menu" copies all 21 recipes and the week's shopping list.

02

Publish it

Publish it as your own app

  1. Download fridge-chef-calm.html from the box above.
  2. Open a new chat in Claude, in a place where Claude can publish an artifact (a live page that stays in your account).
  3. Attach the file to the chat.
  4. Paste the prompt from Step 3 and send it. Don't change it.
  5. When Claude finishes, open the artifact it publishes.
  6. The first time you tap "Spot the ingredients" or "Cook a week of meals", Claude asks permission to use your Claude account, including sending images. Choose Allow.
Private by default

The artifact is private until you share it from its Share menu.

Prompt
I've attached an HTML file for an app called Fridge Chef. Publish it as an Artifact in my own account, exactly as written.

Steps:

1. Read the entire attached file before doing anything. Do NOT rewrite, redesign, reformat, shorten, translate or "improve" any part of it. Publish the file's content byte-for-byte. If something looks unusual, leave it.

2. Publish it with the Artifact tool, as a live artifact (not a downloadable file, not a code block in chat). Settings:
   - Title: Fridge Chef  (the file already contains a <title> tag)
   - Icon: recipe
   - Description: "Your fridge photo in the middle, a week of breakfast, lunch and dinner dishes in your chosen cuisine around it, with a shopping list for the week."
   - Capabilities: {"sample": {"images": true}}
     This capability is required. The page calls Claude from inside itself (window.claude.use("sample")) to read fridge photos and write the recipes. Without it, the page only shows its built-in example menu.

3. Keep the external script tag for the Lucide icon library (https://unpkg.com/lucide@0.344.0/dist/umd/lucide.min.js) exactly as it is. unpkg.com is an allowed artifact CDN. Keep the Google Fonts link too.

4. Do not add any other capabilities (no storage, connectors, downloads or database). The page doesn't use them.

5. Don't run test edits, preview loops or redesign passes. Publish once.

6. After publishing, reply in two or three sentences telling me:
   - that the first time I tap "Spot the ingredients" or "Cook a week of meals", Claude will ask permission to use my Claude account, including sending images, and I need to choose Allow;
   - that the artifact is private until I share it from its Share menu.

Good to know

Before you use it

  • The capability matters. The prompt gives the artifact permission to call Claude from inside the page. Without it, the app only shows its built-in example menu.
  • It uses your Claude account. Reading photos and writing recipes happens through Claude, which is why it asks for permission the first time.
  • A week is a bigger request than a day. Planning 21 dishes may take a little longer, and you can press Stop if you need to.
  • Use 2 to 3 clear photos of your fridge and pantry, with the shelves visible.
  • Check the ingredients list before you cook the week. Remove anything wrong and add anything missed.
  • Check recipes and allergies yourself. The dishes are suggestions generated by AI. Check the method, cooking times and any food allergies before you cook.
